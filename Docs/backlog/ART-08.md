# ART-08 - Outer Decorations for Three-Storey Walls

## Scope & Completion Criteria

Registered 2026-11-23 from [Q-59](../history/Q/Q-59.md): walls may reach three storeys, and three-storey walls get decorations such as banners on their outer face. Start after the storey work (`SIEGE-18` storey limits, `SIEGE-27` interior stair rooms).

- Choose kit decorations (banners and similar) for the outer face of three-storey wall cells and attach them through the castle kit dressing, so they follow building, raising, lowering and collapse. They appear automatically when the dressing condition (three storeys) is met, not as a castle tier unlock (`SIEGE-28`).
- On 7.5m cells kit pieces are stretched 1.5 times along the face (SIEGE-35); ask the user whether decorations stretch or keep the module like the gate frames.
- Keep them clear of ladders, crenels and siege weapon lines of fire; visual only (no colliders that block movement or shots).

Done when three-storey walls show the decorations in the spike and Game scenes, and the dressing rule tests pass.
