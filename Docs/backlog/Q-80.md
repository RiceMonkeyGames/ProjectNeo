# Q-80 — Team Decision: Player Direct Combat Expansion

## Scope & Completion Criteria

Decide the scope, rules, and input layout for expanding first-person player combat beyond the current basic left-click frontal melee attack ([Combat](../design/Combat.md), [Goal](../plan/Goal.md)).

### Decisions Needed

1. **Guard / Block / Parry**:
   - Decide whether to add active blocking (flat/percentage damage reduction while held) and/or timed parrying (staggering melee attackers via `COMBAT-04` poise rules).
   - Decide whether blocking requires an equipped `Offhand` shield or works with any melee weapon, and whether it uses a stamina/poise bar or simple cooldown/interrupt rules.
   - Resolve input conflict: right-click drag in battle is already used for squad formation placement (`COMBAT-08`). Decide the default binding (e.g., separate guard key vs toggle/modifier with formation placement, rebindable in `UI-11`).
2. **Melee Attack Depth**:
   - Choose between keeping simple frontal swings, adding a light/heavy or multi-hit melee combo, or adopting Bannerlord-style directional swings/blocks.
3. **Player Ranged Weapon (Bow) & Weapon Switching**:
   - Decide whether the player can equip/switch to a bow in battle (which also enables player use of gate machicolations from `SIEGE-12`), how ammo/aiming works, and how bow aim coexists with squad drag-placement.
4. **Milestone Timing**:
   - Confirm which items land in M4 vs post-core, then break approved mechanics into `COMBAT-*` tasks.

### Completion Criteria

- Decisions recorded in [Combat](../design/Combat.md) and [Decisions-Resolved](../plan/Decisions-Resolved.md).
- Approved features split into concrete `COMBAT-*` backlog tasks; rejected/postponed items explicitly noted.
