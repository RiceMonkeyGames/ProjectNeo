# SIEGE-14 - Spike: 5m Construction Grid with the Kingdom Castle Kit

## Scope & Completion Criteria

Registered 2026-11-22 from the user's request: keep the `PolygonFantasyKingdom` demo walls at their own proportions. Analysis of `PolygonFantasyKingdom/Scenes/Demo.unity` (3,601 castle instances) showed the kit is a 5m grid: 5x5m wall panels (`Wall_01/03/04/05`, 0.5m thick) on both faces of a bay, `Pillar_Stone_05` at every 5m joint, `Floor_Wood_01` walkway between the panels, `Battlements_01` (1.38m) and `Battlements_Pillar_02` on top, 5m storeys, `Stairs_Straight_Wide_01` 5x5x5m and `Wall_Gate_01` 5.7m. The Kingdom soldier model is 2.0m tall, the same as our units, while the game grid is 3m cells x 3m tiers (0.6 of the kit).

User decision (2026-11-22): before changing the game grid, build a prototype with a 5m grid and kit-dressed wall bays, look at it in play, and list what breaks; then decide with the teammate.

Spike scope (no change to `Game.unity` or shared rule values):

- A separate prototype scene on a 5m grid (5m wall tiers, 5m stairs) built from the construction prototype, where walls can be placed, stacked and removed.
- A visual-only kit dresser that assembles each wall column from kit pieces according to its neighbours (outer and inner panels, joint pillars, walkway floor, battlements on outward top faces) so placing and removing keeps the pattern continuous.
- First-person walk on the walls with the 2m player to judge scale.
- A breakage list: every system that assumes 3m cells or 3m tiers (default castle layout and map, ladders, cover and battlement heights, siege weapon mounts, machicolations, formations on the walkway, costs, tests), with a rough size for each.

Done when the prototype is playable, captures are reviewed with the user, and the breakage list is recorded here or in a history record for the team decision. `ART-07` (box trim on the 3m grid) is parked in a local stash meanwhile.

## Spike result (2026-11-22)

- Scene `Assets/_Project/Scenes/SyntyGridSpike.unity` (menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Create Synty Grid Spike (SIEGE-14)`, rebuilds the scene): a copy of `WallCommandPrototype` on a 16x14 grid of 5m cells, 3 tiers of 5m, 1-cell gate, Synty stairs fitted to 5x5x5m. Tab switches construction / first person.
- `Walls/CastleKitRules` + `Walls/CastleKitDresser` (visual only, rebuilt on every layout change, only changed cells): per wall storey a panel on each face without a same-level neighbour (`Wall_04` on outer faces at even grid indices, else `Wall_01`), a `Pillar_Stone_05` at every used joint, `Floor_Wood_01` on the column top, `Battlements_01` on battlement faces with `Battlements_Pillar_02` caps, `Wall_Gate_01` on the gate cell. Kit prefabs are collider-free copies in `Prefabs/CastleKit` with project URP materials. Collision stays the unit box of each piece.
- Checked in play: the walls match the demo look and proportions next to 2m soldiers; placing three walls (one stacked) went from 11 to 13 dressed cells and dismantling two returned to 12, with pillars and battlements following the new neighbours.
- Seen gaps in the first pass: the gate top had no walkway floor or battlements; an open courtyard gets battlements on both sides of a single-cell wall (rule behaviour, accepted by the user); convex corners use plain joint pillars (no `Wall_Corner_*` pieces yet); collapsed-wall remains are not placed yet.

### Second pass (user requests, 2026-11-22)

- Construction in the spike scene: the HUD lost its wall/stairs links because assets made before opening the scene went stale; the setup now reloads every asset by path after opening the scene. A 5m bastion (`Data/SyntyGridSpike/Bastion.asset`, same prefab and `top` mount as SIEGE-11) and a `Bastion` HUD button were added, so walls, stacking, stairs, bastions and cannons on bastions can all be built (checked through the same `PlacementController` calls the HUD uses).
- Gate: two frames like the demo. The outer face (the gate rotation side) gets `Wall_Gate_01` with its wooden doors and portcullis; the inner face gets the same frame without them. When the gate is breached (`IsDoorOpen`) the doors and portcullis are hidden. The gate top now has a walkway floor and battlements.
- Collapsed walls: `CastleKitDresser` remembers cells whose wall piece collapsed (`ConstructionWorld.PieceCollapsing`, from that level up). Faces toward those cells get no panel, pillar or battlement, leaving room for a later rubble model. Dismantling is not a collapse and re-dresses normally; building on the cell again forgets the breach. Breaches are not saved yet.
- Pillar and pilaster collision: kit copies of `Pillar_Stone_05`, `Wall_04` and the wall stairs keep mesh colliders; the kit container sits under the world `Geometry` root (new read-only `ConstructionWorld.GeometryRoot`) so the NavMesh carves around them and agents no longer overlap them; the NavMesh is rebuilt whenever dressing with colliders changes. Measured: free ground starts about 0.9m from a flat panel, 1.1m in front of a joint pillar and beyond 1.3m in front of a `Wall_04` pilaster.
- Auto stairs: where a wall-like column (wall, bastion, gate) is exactly one storey lower than a neighbour, a `Stairs_Wood_Straight_01` flight (5m rise, 2.16m wide, mesh collider) is placed on the lower walkway against the inner side, rising to the higher top; along parallel high/low rows only the row ends get one. A NavMesh path from the lower walkway to the higher top is complete.
- Third pass (user requests, 2026-11-22): the gate passage gets a `Wall_01` panel on each side between the two frames (facing into the passage), so the hollow between neighbouring wall panels is no longer visible; wall stairs are centred on the walkway so they no longer touch the battlements; courtyard-facing faces use plain `Wall_01` plus a `Wall_Panel_Stone_02` relief on the top storey (anchor 2.75m below the top, as in the demo) instead of the `Wall_04` pilaster accent, which now appears only on outward faces. The spike seed is now a closed ring (cells 3..12 x 3..10, gate on the north row) and the dresser uses the default-castle enclosed rule, so the courtyard stays inside even after a breach.
- Fourth pass (user requests, 2026-11-22):
  - Portcullis and doors: the portcullis is raised during preparation and lowered when battle starts (user decision; the Game wiring comes with integration). `CastleKitDresser.PortcullisRaised` animates it over 1.5s; raised, it folds up into the arch top (it cannot slide straight up without poking through the gate-top walkway). While the gate is held open (`IsHeldOpen`) the two wooden doors swing inward on their hinges; a breached gate hides doors and portcullis.
  - Spike test keys (`Walls/CastleKitTestKeys`, hint line at the bottom of both HUDs): `P` portcullis up/down, `O` hold gate open/close, `B` breach/repair gate, `K` collapse the wall or bastion at the screen centre.
  - Collapsed-wall look, following the demo's broken walls: the end cap between the two panel rows is `DestroyedWall_Rubble_Top_01` on the column's top storey, `Rubble_Middle_01` on middle storeys and `Rubble_Bottom_01` on the ground storey, with a `DestroyedWall_RubblePile_01` on the breach floor; on the last intact bay the half of each face panel next to the breach becomes a `DestroyedWall_Top/Middle/Bottom_01` fragment (same anchor as the cap, as in the demo) and the other half a `Wall_Cut_01`; its battlement keeps only the far half (`Battlements_01_Half`); no joint pillars or caps on the broken end. All visual only, so the breach stays passable. Open: the walkway floor of the end bay still reaches the breach.
- Fifth pass (user request, 2026-11-22): the gate column is two storeys by default (gate + a fixed `Wall` placed with `fixedGate` so it cannot be dismantled). With a storey above, the portcullis model slides straight up (`portcullisLift` 4.6m module) into the upper outer panel and is hidden; the fold-up fallback is used only if the gate column has no upper storey (for example after that storey collapses). The gate gets a wooden passage ceiling under the upper storey, and the auto stairs lead from both neighbouring walkways to the gatehouse top.
- Sixth pass (user chose option A for awkward stairs, 2026-11-22): a low corner cell (walkway turns, one storey below a neighbour) gets the quarter-turn `Stairs_Wood_Corner_01` starting from the incoming walkway and ending at the higher top, mirrored in z when the turn goes the other way; it is stretched 1.12x horizontally because the original flights (about 48.7° and 45°) are steeper than the agent slope limit (46°), and the NavMesh path from the walkway to the higher top is complete. A valley cell (both ends one storey higher) gets two straight stairs on opposite halves, which no longer meet in a V, but the NavMesh shows each flight as an island: each foot sits against the opposite high wall and the gap between the two flights is about 0.3m, so a valley cannot connect both sides with stairs inside one 5m cell. Valley handling is pending a user decision.
- Seventh pass (user decisions, 2026-11-22): valleys are forbidden instead of dressed. `BuildGridDefinition.forbidValleys` (spike grid only) makes `BuildLayout.Check` return the new `PlacementIssue.Valley` (=12) when a placement would leave a neighbour lower than both cells on one axis, `BuildLayout.ValleyFill` tells how many pieces a gap needs and `ConstructionWorld.TryPlace` stacks that many at once, `TryDismantle` refuses removals that would leave a valley (`RemovalMakesValley`), and `ConstructionWorld.Collapse` destroys every storey above the collapsed piece instead of dropping it. The spike seed row 8..11 is two storeys next to the gatehouse (no valley). Broken ends now follow the demo orientation: rubble caps and wall fragments are anchored at the middle of the end bay with their local +x toward the breach, so the wall steps down toward the gap and the caps close the hollow; the end bay keeps only the intact half of its walkway floor (`Floor_Wood_03`).
- Eighth pass (user request, 2026-11-22, supersedes the end-bay breakage of the fourth and seventh passes): neighbours of a collapsed cell stay intact (full panels, floor, battlements and pillars; only their face toward the gap is left open), and the ruin sits inside the collapsed cell as a V, like the demo's broken wall: from each wall-like neighbour, a `DestroyedWall_Top_01` fragment on both wall faces and a `DestroyedWall_Rubble_Top_01` cap between them descend from the neighbour's top at the joint to the ground toward the cell centre (stretched vertically by the collapsed height, 0.7 of half a cell wide so about 1.5m of floor stays open in the middle), with a low rubble pile (0.5 height) in the middle. Visual only; a NavMesh path from outside through the breach to the courtyard is complete for both a one-storey and a two-storey collapse. `RubbleMiddle/Bottom`, `PanelHalf`, `BrokenMiddle/Bottom`, `BattlementHalf` and `FloorHalf` kit pieces are no longer placed.
- Ninth pass (user request, 2026-11-23): stairs whose top lands on a collapsed wall cell stay and keep closing the gap; the breach cell gets one more `DestroyedWall_Rubble_Top_01` cap on the stairs' end face, stretched to the stair top (no broken wall panels on that side, since the neighbours' V halves already cover the sides). Spike stairs have 50 HP (walls 100) so attackers can break through; key K now also collapses aimed stairs. Enemy targeting of blocking stairs is teammate AI work at integration.
- Tenth pass (user report, 2026-11-23): a rebuilt wall still showed a broken-wall V half and section on its outer face. Cause: the bastion in front of it had collapsed first (key K from outside hits the bastion), and its cell was remembered as a wall breach, so the V half leaned on the wall and the wall's face toward it stayed hidden. Fix: a collapsed bastion is remembered as a rubble-only cell (`CastleKitDresser.Rubble`, `CastleKitRules.Collect(..., rubble)`): a low rubble pile and nothing on the wall behind it, cleared when anything is built there. Seeded bastions are also left out of the dresser's always-inside set, so the wall face toward a fallen bastion is dressed as an outer face with battlements.
- Tests: EditMode `CastleKitRulesTests` (16), `ValleyRuleTests` (4). Play mode in the background editor needs `editor.playmode set_run_in_background --enabled true`, otherwise frames stall and the NavMesh never rebuilds.

## Breakage list if the game grid moves to 5m (size: S small, M medium, L large; owner in brackets)

1. Map and default castle (`Editor/GameFlowSetup`: 80x100 cells of 3m, ring cells 24..55 / 28..59, gate column 39, courtyard facility and squad cells): becomes 48x60 cells; the 96m ring must become 95m or 100m. M (youngho).
2. Saved runs store castle cells per slot (FLOW-02, META-10 migration): old saves need a migration or a reset. M (youngho).
3. Tier height 3m to 5m: 3 tiers = 15m. Q-01 chose two-tier 6m default walls; on 5m one tier (5m) is already demo height, so the default should be re-decided. Fall damage values (`FallSettings`) and jump height checks need tuning. S/M.
4. Battlements and cover (`BattlementSettings` 1.1m sill / 2.2m merlons vs kit `Battlements_01` 1.38m with a ~0.7m crenel sill): cover rules, crouch-reload (COMBAT-10), crenel stand points, ladder top (`SillHeightAt`) and `ShotCover` colliders must follow the kit or the kit must be scaled. L (teammate, needs a rule decision).
5. Siege ladders: lengths, raise/climb timing and slot spacing (about every 5 cells, now 25m apart) for 5m-10m walls. M (teammate).
6. Main gate: 2x1 placeholder of 3m cells becomes a 1-cell 5m `Wall_Gate_01`; door collider, breach logic and machicolation sizes (`MachicolationSettings` 3m shaft, overhang) follow. M (teammate).
7. Bastions (SIEGE-11, just merged): bastion piece and mount points sized for 3m cells. M (teammate).
8. Stairs: one cell, 5m over 5m keeps the same 45° slope, so walking rules hold; Q-59 stair constraints unchanged. S.
9. Walkway width 3m to 5m: formation rank limits (COMBAT-08) and crenel slots give more room; check spacing. S (teammate).
10. Balance per piece (one 5m piece covers about 2.8 times the area of a 3m piece): wall HP, build/repair cost and time, wall-breaker damage, cannon blast radius vs cells. S/M.
11. Construction camera zoom and grid lines (`BuildCameraSettings`). S.
12. Other prototype scenes (`ConstructionPrototype`, `SiegePrototype`, `WallCommandPrototype`) stay on 3m unless regenerated. S.
13. Tests: about 10 Game PlayMode test files use Game cells or heights; EditMode tests mostly build their own grids. M.
14. Kit dressing to finish: corner pieces, collapsed remains (`DestroyedWall_*`) and whether breaches are saved, battlement colliders matching the kit, replacing `CastleBattlements` boxes (gate top, gate frames, wall stairs and pillar collision are done in the spike). M (youngho).
15. Draw cost: about 10 kit instances per dressed bay (a 2-tier ring of ~120 bays is ~2,500 objects); consider static batching or mesh combining per cell. S/M.
16. Follow-on: `ART-07` box trim becomes unnecessary; `SIEGE-13` (battlement slicing, 6m gate) is replaced by the kit pieces.
