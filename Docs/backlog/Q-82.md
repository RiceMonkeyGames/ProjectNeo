# Q-82 — Team Decision: Hero Traits, Enemy Archetypes, Prisoner Rewards & Deferred Morale Rules

## Scope & Completion Criteria

Resolve the remaining mid-tier unit, enemy, reward, and squad-morale design hooks currently deferred across [Combat](../design/Combat.md), [WallsAndSiege](../design/WallsAndSiege.md), [MoraleAndWave](../design/MoraleAndWave.md), and [PrepEconomyMeta](../design/PrepEconomyMeta.md).

### Decisions Needed

1. **Hero Passive Traits & Unique Skills**:
   - Decide whether heroes gain passive traits (e.g., automatic wave scouting reveal, increased `Awaken` resolve chance, passive squad aura) alongside their 3 grade-unlocked active skills.
   - Coordinate with `Q-61` on whether each M4 hero receives unique skills or draws from an expanded shared skill library.
2. **Enemy Archetypes & Enemy Morale**:
   - Decide whether to add non-boss elite enemy heroes and/or human enemy factions that use the dormant `MoraleSide.Enemy` path (`Q-44`), or keep all enemies morale-free monsters.
3. **Prisoner Rescue Rewards**:
   - Decide whether victory rewards include rescued prisoners (free post-battle grunt reinforcements, [Goal](../plan/Goal.md), [PrepEconomyMeta](../design/PrepEconomyMeta.md)) or formally drop the feature.
4. **Deferred Squad & Morale Rules**:
   - Decide `Q-45` (whether to add minimum squad size or automatic merging of under-strength squads, or keep current manual organization with persistent empty squads from `UI-07`).
   - Decide whether player proximity grants a morale bonus/protection to nearby allied squads ([MoraleAndWave](../design/MoraleAndWave.md)).

### Completion Criteria

- Each item is explicitly approved (with rules written into `Docs/design/`) or dropped, recorded in [Decisions-Resolved](../plan/Decisions-Resolved.md).
- Approved features split into concrete `COMBAT-*` / `PREP-*` / `CONTENT-*` tasks.
