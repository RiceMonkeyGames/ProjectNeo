# Q-83 — Team Decision: Audio Asset Sourcing, Style & Voice Bark Scope

## Scope & Completion Criteria

Establish the audio direction and asset sourcing rules before wiring production SFX and BGM (`AUDIO-02`, `AUDIO-03`; [Milestones](../plan/Milestones.md), [OpenQuestions](../design/OpenQuestions.md)).

### Decisions Needed

1. **Asset Sourcing & Submodule Storage**:
   - Choose the source for medieval/fantasy SFX and BGM (licensed Asset Store packs added to `Assets/ThirdParty` in `ProjectNeo-Assets`, royalty-free/CC0 packs, or custom audio).
   - Confirm file-size limits (each file under GitHub's 100 MB limit in `ProjectNeo-Assets`, [Collaboration](../workflow/Collaboration.md)).
2. **Voice Barks & Callouts**:
   - Decide whether squad commands (`Move`/`Attack`/`Hold`), morale events (`Awaken`/`Collapse`/rout), player/hero skills, and courtyard NPCs use voice barks/shouts or stay strictly non-vocal SFX (horns, drums, chimes).
3. **Music Structure & Dynamic Layering**:
   - Decide whether BGM uses simple per-phase tracks (Title, Preparation, Battle, Victory/Defeat stingers) or adds dynamic escalation layers (boss wave entry, main gate breach).

### Completion Criteria

- Audio pack/source selection, voice scope, and BGM layering rules recorded in [Decisions-Resolved](../plan/Decisions-Resolved.md) and [ArtAssets](../codemap/ArtAssets.md) (or a dedicated audio asset map).
- Unblocks `AUDIO-02` and `AUDIO-03`.
