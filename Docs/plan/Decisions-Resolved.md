## Resolved `Q-*` Table

Ranged occlusion & melee spread (COMBAT-12, 2026-11-22, user): (1) a ranged `Engage` unit whose shot is blocked by a living enemy switches to that enemy when it is in range and can be shot, otherwise keeps its firing distance and waits; structure blocking keeps the old walk-closer behavior; (2) melee attackers take the nearest free slot on a ring around the target, skipping off-NavMesh slots, falling back to the centre when none is usable; (3) when the ring is full, extra melee attackers wait on an outer ring; (4) applies to allies and enemies alike. Rules: [Combat](../design/Combat.md).

Machicolations (SIEGE-12, 2026-11-22, user): (1) fixed openings on the main gate top only, present from the start and free; other wall pieces are a later extension; (2) used by both allied AI units and the player; (3) attack methods are swappable modules, SIEGE-12 ships only arrows straight down (stones, oil later); (4) method chosen automatically by attacker type, no UI; (5) allied ranged AI in `Hold` near a free opening walks to it when an enemy is below and nothing else is visible, then returns to its post. Rules: [WallsAndSiege](../design/WallsAndSiege.md).

Synty packs and piece specs (Q-01, 2026-11-22, user, agent recommendation accepted): (1) packs: keep what is already applied and fix one main pack per role: castle and buildings `PolygonFantasyKingdom`; allied units `PolygonFantasyKingdom` and `PolygonElvenRealm`, with `PolygonKnights` as the supplement; enemies `PolygonFantasyRivals`, with `Synty/PolygonGoblinWarCamp` and `PolygonDarkFantasy` as supplements; UI `Synty/InterfaceFantasyWarriorHUD` (UI-05/UI-10); animation `Synty/AnimationBaseLocomotion`, `Kevin Iglesias` and the ExplosiveLLC archer walk (ART-04); (2) piece specs: keep 3m grid cells and 3m tiers (no 5m Synty module); battlements reuse the Synty battlement meshes sliced into repeat modules and tiled along each face at the game pattern (SIEGE-13 approach); whether cover heights stay 1.1m / 2.2m (meshes stretched about 1.6x vertically) or move toward Synty proportions stays open and is decided at SIEGE-13 start with the teammate, since it changes cover rules; (3) default castle walls become two stacked tiers (6m) and the main gate keeps `SM_Bld_Castle_Wall_Gate_01` proportions at 6m; implemented in SIEGE-13. Every applied third-party asset is listed in the [ArtAssets codemap](../codemap/ArtAssets.md) so a model is not reused for two roles.

Save migration details (META-10, 2026-11-21, user): (1) removed content is refunded at the full price paid (items: buy price plus upgrade gold; heroes: hire cost, gear to the stash; soldiers: recruit cost; castle pieces and siege weapons: build cost; meta unlock entries: unlock points); shop/tavern offers are restocked, perks return their point, equipped skills are cleared; (2) developer log only, no player notice; (3) renames in one shared asset (kind, old id, new id); (4) a removed player hero is swapped for the current default player hero, keeping progress and gear. At start (same day): (5) every save records each bought thing's value at that save, since removed content has no price to read (older saves refund 0); (6) the refund includes the material part of costs; (7) the meta save records unlock points spent per opened entry (older entries get the current cost, legacy gacha imports 0). Rules: [PrepEconomyMeta](../design/PrepEconomyMeta.md).

Wall-top cover fire (COMBAT-10, 2026-11-21, user): (1) sill 0.8m -> 1.1m for Lv1 and Lv2; (2) only AI-controlled allied ranged units behind their own battlement crouch to reload, rising 0.5s before the next shot (crouched height 55%); (3) at Lv2 a ranged squad's front rank snaps to the nearest crenels and leftovers form rear ranks; (4) enemy archers unchanged; (5) after playtest, archer attack speed 1.0 -> 0.4 (ranged soldiers and hero archer; damage and Marksman unchanged). COMBAT-04 (2026-11-16): User approved a data-driven single-hit poise vs stagger threshold system (`IsInterruptible && incomingStagger >= actionPoise`).
