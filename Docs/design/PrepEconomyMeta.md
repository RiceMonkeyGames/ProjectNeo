## Preparation Phase & Facilities

- **Default Perspective** (2026-11-11): 1st-person courtyard navigation. Facing a building and pressing `F` opens its modal pop-up (`Esc` or Close exits; movement/look frozen; disabled during combat). Construction station switches to orthographic build mode on `F`, `Esc` returns to 1st-person at matching pose.
- **Construction return-hint panel input** (QA-build-zoom-ui-hover, user decision 2026-11-14): Block wheel camera zoom over the bottom `건설 모드 · Esc 돌아가기` panel, including its background. World wheel zoom resumes after leaving the panel, as with the left construction controls.

| Facility | Function | Upgrade Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Tavern | Hire randomly appearing heroes | Higher tier heroes appear |
| Shop | Buy/sell items; randomized inventory | Inventory variety expands |
| Barracks | Recruit and train grunts | Grunt stat enhancements |
| Blacksmith | Craft, upgrade, sell hero equipment | Higher tier equipment crafting |
| Siege | Wall/weapon management: add, mount, repair, reinforce | Facility-specific data modules |

- **Refresh & Upgrades** (2026-11-12): Shop and Tavern refresh inventories on entering each prep phase (gold re-rolling is a follow-up). Facility upgrades pay next-level gold; Shop tier reflects on next prep phase (prevents free in-phase re-roll).
- **Shop & Equipment Prototypes** (2026-11-12): Shop rolls from tier pools excluding locked items; purchased items leave stock; sell price = `50%` of purchase gold (data-configurable, applies to combat/reward items too). Press `I` during prep for player equipment UI (3 generic slots, immediate stat updates; hero equipment follows Tavern).
- **Blacksmith** (Q-35, 2026-11-13): *Fixed recipes* — every unlocked equipment recipe allowed by the current blacksmith level (recipe list per level in data; higher levels add higher-tier gear) is always craftable, with no random roll. Cost = Gold + Monster Materials from recipe data. *Upgrades* — each equipment item carries its own level (+1 to +N); each level adds +10% to that item's equip stat modifiers; max level by blacksmith level Lv1 +1 / Lv2 +3 / Lv3 +5; step cost = item gold price × 50% × target level, plus materials (all data-configurable); always succeeds. *Selling* — same rule as Shop (equipment, `50%` of purchase gold); upgraded items also refund the same ratio of gold spent on upgrades.
- **Upgrade integration details** (PREP-01, user decision 2026-11-14): Prototype upgrade materials = crafting recipe materials × target upgrade level, stored on each item per target level. Equipped equipment must be unequipped into the stash before upgrading. Shared integration resumes after PREP-03 completion.
- **Upgrade material ownership** (PREP-01, user decision 2026-11-13): Store required materials per target upgrade level on `ItemDefinition`, alongside the item's other data. Common gold ratio, modifier bonus and smithy level limits belong to shared upgrade settings. Implement independent upgrade rules first; defer shared run/shop/facility UI integration until PREP-02 is ready, as agreed by the user.
- **Shop Re-roll & Locks** (Q-46, 2026-11-13): Pay gold to re-roll the current offers; cost rises with each re-roll in one prep phase (e.g., 10g, 20g, 30g; data) and resets each prep phase. Locking is free; max locked offers = Shop level. Locked offers survive re-rolls and the next prep restock; buying a locked offer removes its lock. *Details (user, 2026-11-13)*: cost keeps rising by the same step with no cap and no count limit (10, 20, 30, 40…); a re-roll refills every offer slot, including ones already bought this prep; each row gets its own lock button (row click stays "buy"); the lock cap follows the current Shop level immediately after an upgrade; a newly rolled offer may duplicate a locked item. A re-roll draws from the Shop level that stocked this prep, so an upgrade still shows from the next prep (existing upgrade rule kept).
- **Consumables** (PREP-04, 2026-11-15; choices delegated to the agent by the user, open to revision after playtest and with the Q-11 key layout): During battle the player presses `Q` (data binding in `GameBattleSettings`) to use one healing consumable from the run stash on themself. *Effect*: instantly restores `UseHealRatio` of maximum HP (item data; potion 35%), never above maximum. *Limits*: battle phase only, living player only, not at full HP (nothing is consumed), shared 10s cooldown that resets when the battle ends; no carry limit beyond what the stash holds. *Selection*: no quick-slot UI yet; the oldest usable consumable is used, and the battle status panel shows key, name, count and remaining cooldown. Heroes and grunts do not use consumables. Skills and other consumable effects remain with Q-12.
- **Barracks** (Q-37, 2026-11-13): Training is automatic by barracks level (no separate paid training action), granting stat bonuses to all grunts immediately on upgrade (Lv1 none, Lv2 +10% max HP/attack, Lv3 +20% max HP/attack). Melee (15g) and ranged (20g) grunts recruitable from Lv1 (data asset values).
- **Unit Deployment** (Q-36, 2026-11-13):
  - *Barracks recruits*: Pick target squad in UI (default: first with free space). If all full, auto-creates a new squad up to cap, then blocks.
  - *Tavern heroes*: Pick squad to captain; swaps existing captain to reserve (excluded from battle until D-8 reorganization).
  - *Scene spawn*: Recruited/hired units spawn in front of their facility during current prep and walk to their squad.
- **Squad Organization** (Q-45, Q-47, 2026-11-13): Press `O` during prep exploration to open the organization window (movement/look frozen like `I`; `Esc` closes; disabled in combat). Left column lists the chosen source squad's grunts, right column picks the target squad; clicking a grunt moves that one grunt (blocked when the target is full). Captains are assigned from reserve heroes or unassigned (back to reserve); a hero made captain walks to its squad's gather point (BUG-hired-unit-not-joining-squad, 2026-11-20), while an unassigned captain stays where it stands. Merging is manual only: no automatic merge or minimum size; a squad emptied this way is removed and its captain returns to reserve; the receiving squad keeps its morale. Changes apply to scene squads and battle commands immediately. *Layout revision (Q-49, 2026-11-13, UI-01)*: replace the two-column window with side-by-side squad cards (captain slot + grunts), a new-squad card and a reserve-hero card; click a unit to select, then click the destination card.
- **Player as Captain** (Q-32, final user decision 2026-11-19; interim since 2026-11-13): Not allowed. The player stays an independent unit and cannot be assigned as a squad captain; no player-captain feature is planned.
- **Tavern Heroes** (Q-48, 2026-11-13): Hire cost is gold per hero tier in hero data (prototype 60 / 120 / 200g). Reserve heroes are excluded from battle for now; keep deployment extensible so a reserve hero can later be placed into a captain-less squad. Hero equipment is equipped through the `I` window with a player/hero selector (part of D-3).
- **Hero Grade & Promotion** (Q-73, team decision 2026-11-19; PREP-10): A hero's grade rises when its battle EXP is full and the player pays a promotion cost at a new preparation facility (training ground; name, cost and EXP values in data). A hero starts at the grade of its hire tier (tavern tiers 60/120/200g). Each hero has 3 fixed skills in its data; each grade opens the next one (grade 1: 1 skill, 2: 2, 3: 3).
- **Pre-Battle Squad Placement** (PREP-05, user approved 2026-11-15): In preparation exploration the battle squad keys work (F1–F4 / ` select, 1 move, 2 attack, 3 hold). `P` opens a separate top-down **squad placement view** (construction camera, no build tools; `P` or Esc returns): select with F1–F4 / ` or by clicking a unit, left-click ground or a wall top to move, right-click clears the selection. Units walk. During preparation, destinations must lie inside the rectangle covered by wall and gate pieces (wall tops and courtyard included; outer field rejected with a message); battle orders are unrestricted. Each squad remembers its last placement made during preparation (battle orders do not overwrite it) and walks back there when the next preparation phase starts; positions now outside the castle or off the NavMesh are forgotten. In preparation the battle key-help box is hidden and the squad keys appear as one line in the bottom preparation hint (single source in `GameFlowHud.PreparationSquadHint`, to be refined later).

### Preparation Time (Q-53, user decision 2026-11-18)

Each preparation phase gets a **time budget** shown as a clock. Physical work costs time; trading and organizing do not. All numbers below are starting values held in data and tuned after playtests.

- **Budget**: `12` hours per preparation phase. Unused hours are lost; every preparation starts with the full budget.
- **No time of day or calendar** (Q-55, team decision 2026-11-19): this budget is the only in-game time. Battles have no clock, and there is no day/night cycle or day count.
- **What advances the clock** (thinking time is unlimited; the clock never runs on its own):
  - Crafting or upgrading one item at the blacksmith: `1` hour.
  - Scouting (see [Wave Preview](MoraleAndWave.md#wave-preview)): `3` hours. The scouting action itself is deferred (user, 2026-11-18, C-5) and not yet playable; it is tracked as PREP-07, unblocked by Q-55 (2026-11-19).
  - **Wait**: `1` hour per use, so ordered work can progress. Input (user, 2026-11-18, PREP-06): in first-person exploration hold `T` for `1` second (progress shown like the `B` battle hold); each wait needs a fresh press.
- **Clock display** (user, 2026-11-18, PREP-06): one line at the top center (`Prep time 11h left / 12h`, `- out of time` when exhausted), visible in every preparation view and above facility/equipment windows; hidden in battle, reward and result screens, and while the nearly full-screen squad organization window is open. Actions that cost time show their hours (`· 1h`) and refuse with `Not enough time.` without spending anything.
- **Free actions** (no clock cost, still allowed after the clock runs out): shop buy/sell/re-roll/lock, tavern hiring, barracks recruiting, facility upgrades, equipment, squad organization, squad placement, placing work orders. Battle still starts only when the player holds `B` (FLOW-01).
- **Work orders (construction and repair)**: Placing a piece or ordering a repair costs gold as today but no clock time; it becomes a work order. A **work crew** builds it while the clock advances. `2` crews work at once on orders in placement order; each wall, stair or weapon piece needs `1` hour of work; repairing a piece needs its missing-HP fraction of `1` hour (a destroyed piece = `1` hour). Increasing the crew count through upgrades is a later idea, not part of this rule.
- **Demolishing**: still free; demolishing a piece still under construction refunds its full cost.
- **Clock exhausted** (`12` hours used): clock actions and work progress stop; free actions continue.
- **Unfinished work at battle start**: a piece under construction stands with HP equal to its progress (50% built = 50% HP) and blocks paths like a finished piece. It continues as a normal damaged piece and can be repaired in later preparations. Unfinished repairs keep the HP reached so far.
- This replaces the open "construction delays" question (SIEGE-08); there is no separate construction delay during battle.
- **Work order details** (user, 2026-11-18, SIEGE-08):
  - *Not started at battle start*: a build order with `0%` progress disappears at battle start and its gold is refunded in full.
  - *Stacking*: pieces can be stacked and cannons mounted **only on finished pieces**; an unfinished piece rejects anything on top.
  - *Repair gold*: paid in full when the repair is ordered (`Repair All`, as before); at battle start the unfinished share is refunded (gold × remaining fraction, rounded down) and the HP reached so far is kept.
  - *Unfinished cannon*: enters battle with HP equal to its progress but **cannot fire** (auto-fire and manning are off) until it is complete. It stays unfinished across battles; repairing it to full HP in a later preparation completes it.
  - *Broken gate*: a repair order closes the gate only when the repair is finished; if it is still unfinished at battle start, the gate closes then with the HP reached so far.
  - *Display*: a piece or cannon under construction is shown translucent with its progress (`40%`) above it, and the construction screen lists the work queue summary.

## Currencies, Items & Rewards

- **Currencies**: `Gold` (primary), `Monster Materials` (secondary, multiple sub-types dropped by defeated monsters = "Material Items"), `Items` (equipment and consumables; equipment shared interchangeably between player and heroes).
- **Dual-slot Costs**: All costs hold Gold + Materials (unused slots = 0).
- **Victory Rewards** (2026-11-12): Randomized Gold (base + stage scaling), Monster Materials & bounty gold per kill, drop-chance Finished Equipment, Commander EXP. Granted once per victory. (Unlock points are no longer a victory reward; they are granted at run end, Q-76.) Prisoner rescue rewards deferred until recruitment is online; Unlock point spending: Darkest Dungeon 2-style tracks (Q-76).

## Meta Progression

Two elements survive run failure: **Perk Point Count** (spent allocations reset on death; pool persists for re-allocation) and **Unlocks** (heroes/items persist even if hero dies), together with unspent unlock currency.

- **Commander Level & Perks**: EXP gained from stage clears only (the reincarnation bonus was dropped, Q-71, 2026-11-19). Perks belong to the player (commander) only; heroes have no perks. New player skills are obtained with perk points earned on Commander level-up (Q-66; perk direction Q-72). Perks are allocated only during preparation and on the reward screen, never in battle, so no pause is needed (Q-06, team decision 2026-11-19). Categories: `Combat` (player), `Economy` (gold/items/prep), `Unit Enhancement` (squads/heroes), including stat buffs and special traits (e.g., morale boost). Perk structure (Q-72, 2026-11-19): Three perk trees by the existing categories: Combat (player), Economy (gold, items, preparation) and Unit Enhancement (squads, heroes). Skill-granting perks are entries in the Combat tree. Commander level-up gives 1 perk point. Skills learned through perks reset with the perk allocation when a run ends and are chosen again next run. When the player knows more than 3 skills, 3 are equipped during preparation.
- **Unlocks** (Q-76, team decision 2026-11-19; replaces the gacha and the Q-07 pity rule): Between-run unlocks follow a Darkest Dungeon 2-style model instead of a gacha: unlock currency is earned through the run and kept on defeat; between runs the player spends it on the Title/meta screen to open fixed entries of their choice, with no randomness or duplicates. Entries sit on one straight track per category (open in order, cost rising along the track, e.g. 1 -> 2 -> 3 -> 5 -> 8); the data model is entry + prerequisites so branching trees or per-hero unlocks can be added later. No refunds. Entries currently flagged as guaranteed initial appearances become unlocked by default. Perks stay on Commander level and are not part of the unlock tracks. Currency source (Q-76, team 2026-11-19): unlock points are granted **once, when a run ends** (defeat or the ending after the last stage), from how far the run got (stage reached, bosses killed; amounts in data), multiplied by the Ascension level multiplier (Q-78); the per-victory unlock point reward (C-4) is removed. Tracks (Q-76, team 2026-11-19): four categories for now: **Heroes** (heroes that can appear in the tavern), **Equipment recipes** (blacksmith recipes), **Shop items** (items and consumables the shop can stock) and **Starting bonuses** (starting gold, grunts, consumables). Facility max level, walls/cannons, work crews, prep hours, squads and morale are not unlock tracks for now. First entries and costs are data, proposed with META-07. Entry model (META-05, user decision 2026-11-19): every entry has its own id (e.g. `recipe_steel_sword`), separate from the hero or item it opens, so the same item can sit on both the Equipment recipes and Shop items tracks and starting bonuses need no content id; what an entry opens is a payload of kind + target id + amount. Order is stored as an explicit list of prerequisite entry ids on each entry; a straight track lists only the previous entry, and an entry with several prerequisites needs all of them. The old "newly unlocked appears for sure next run" rule of the gacha is dropped. First tracks (META-07, user approval 2026-11-19; costs are data in `Data/GameFlow/Unlocks/`): **Heroes** Swordsman and Archer open by default, Knight 2 -> Paladin 3; **Equipment recipes** Wood Shield, Iron Sword and Leather Armor open by default, Chain Armor 1 -> Steel Sword 2; **Shop items** Potion, Wood Shield, Iron Sword and Leather Armor open by default, Chain Armor 1 -> Steel Sword 2; **Starting bonuses** gold +50 (1) -> potions x2 (2) -> grunts +2 (3) -> gold +100 (5). How run content reads them (META-07): a hero, recipe or shop item is available only when an entry that opens it is opened; content that no track entry opens stays available (user decision 2026-11-19); the facility level still decides which content its pool offers. Opened starting bonuses add up and are granted once when a new run starts (not again when a saved run continues); starting grunts are the first soldier type the barracks can recruit at its starting level. The `unlockedByDefault` flag on hero and item data no longer decides availability once tracks are present; it still applies to perks. Unlock screen (META-06, user decisions 2026-11-19): the Title screen has an `Unlocks` button that opens the selected slot's unlock window over the title; it shows the unlock point balance and the four tracks side by side, each entry as Opened, its cost, or its cost with the entries it still needs. Clicking an entry once asks for confirmation (the button reads `Click again`); clicking the same entry again buys it, and the slot is saved right away. Refusals (already opened, missing prerequisite, not enough points) are explained in a status line and spend nothing. The slot buttons now show the unlock point balance.
- **Run score** (Q-70, 2026-11-19): stage cleared 1,000 each; boss killed 500 each; enemy killed 10 each; main gate intact at a victory 200 per stage; at run end 100 per living hero, 10 per living grunt and 1 per 10 gold held; +5,000 for reaching the ending; the run total is multiplied by the Ascension level multiplier x(1 + 0.1 x level) (Q-78, 2026-11-20, value in data; replaces the post-ending loop multiplier (1 + 0.5 x k)). A defeat keeps the points earned so far. Shown on the ending and defeat result screens; it no longer feeds Commander EXP (Q-71).
- **Meta Save** (META-01, 2026-11-16): Meta progression (commander level/XP, perk point count, unlock points, opened unlock entries, and the older content-id unlock list kept until META-07) is saved to disk in one of **three slots** chosen on the Title screen. It is saved right after every meta change and once more when the application quits. Each save keeps the previous file as a backup; an unreadable file falls back to the backup, and if both are unreadable the damaged files are kept under another name and the slot starts fresh. A slot can be deleted from the Title screen with a second confirming click; deleting a slot also deletes its run save.
- **Run Save & Continue** (FLOW-02, 2026-11-16; choices delegated to the agent, open to revision): Each slot also holds **one run in progress**. The run is saved automatically — there is no manual save — at four moments, each of which can be switched off in one place: on entering a preparation phase, right before a battle starts, right after a victory reward is granted (saved as the next stage's preparation), and when the application quits during preparation. **Battles are never saved**: quitting mid-battle returns to that battle's preparation exactly as it was left (purchases, construction, organization kept; the battle is replayed). Quitting on the reward screen continues at the next stage's preparation and cannot grant the same reward twice. **Defeat deletes the run save**; there is nothing to continue after a lost run. The Title screen shows `Continue (Stage N)` only when the selected slot has a run save; `New Game` on such a slot needs a second confirming click and then discards the saved run. Saved: stage number, run seed, gold and materials, stash and equipped items with upgrade levels, heroes (including fallen ones), soldiers and squad organization with captains and squad morale, facility levels, shop offers/locks/re-roll count and tavern offers, castle pieces with health and mounted weapons. Not saved: unit health (everyone is healed at preparation anyway), unit positions and remembered deployment spots (units stand with their squad or in front of the barracks/tavern), squad orders. Run saves follow the meta save's backup and damaged-file rules. A run save that can no longer be restored (for example an item that no longer exists) is set aside under another name and a new run starts at stage 1.
- **Balance Risks**: Mitigate pool dilution via tavern re-rolls/locked slots; mitigate power creep via point caps and trait-oriented perks.
