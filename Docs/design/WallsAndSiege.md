# Game Design: Castle Walls, Enemies & Siege Strategy

Last Updated: 2026-11-21 16:53:10 (KST)

> Purpose: Construction stacking, build economy, enemy roles, and siege/ladder rules. | Parent: [design/README](README.md) | Related: [Combat](Combat.md), [MoraleAndWave](MoraleAndWave.md)

## Castle Walls & Construction

- **One-cell stairs (approved 2026-11-13, Q-42; SIEGE-03 Done; user accepted stair feel)**: Stairs occupy 1×1 grid cell (3×3m) and ascend one full tier (3m), replacing the former 1×2 footprint. The placeholder remains a continuous ramp. All four rotations support player and unit ascent/descent, including stairs stacked on walls. Existing scene placements retain their high landing positions. No stacking on stairs; existing construction costs are retained. [Implementation and verification](../history/SIEGE/SIEGE-03.md).
- **Battle start (approved 2026-11-13, Q-40)**: Player moves to the desired position during preparation and starts battle there. Battle start must not require returning to the gate banner or automatically relocate the player. FLOW-01 input approved: hold B continuously for 1 second in preparation exploration; show key hint and hold progress. Release or leave exploration to cancel; construction/facility/equipment panels block starting. Preserve position and view, and start only once. The old gate-banner F trigger is retired.

- **Map scale standard** (approved 2026-11-12): Total ground 240×300m, castle w/ outer walls 96×96m, courtyard 90×90m, northern approach 120m; 3m grid cells, up to 3 vertical tiers. One fixed main gate ("Wall Breached" on destruction). *Q-01 (user, 2026-11-22)*: cells and tiers stay 3m; the default castle walls become two stacked tiers (6m) and the main gate a 6m gate keeping `SM_Bld_Castle_Wall_Gate_01` proportions (superseded below). *Team decision (2026-11-23)*: the Game moves to 5m cells and 5m tiers built with the `PolygonFantasyKingdom` castle kit (SIEGE-14 spike); the ground size and ring size (96m becomes 95m or 100m) are re-decided in `SIEGE-18`. *Q-59 (user, 2026-11-23)*: on the 5m grid walls, bastions and the gatehouse column reach at most 3 storeys (15m) and towers 4 storeys (20m), so the Game grid has 4 levels with storey 4 for tower tiers only; a tower beside the default walls is 2 storeys; the run-start castle walls are one storey (5m), using the same modular kit pieces and dressing as player-built walls (no separate default-wall assets; the shared modular dressing itself may be improved, e.g. by `SIEGE-27`); the gatehouse stays two storeys. Implementation `SIEGE-18`. *SIEGE-35 (user, 2026-11-23, after the 7.5m spike)*: the Game grid uses **7.5m × 7.5m cells with 5m storeys** (cell width and storey height are separate; every height above stays in 5m storeys). Kit pieces keep their 5m module height and thickness and are **stretched 1.5× along the face** (panels, accents, battlements, doors, arrow slits, corbels; floors 1.5× on both horizontal axes), except gate frames, which keep their 5m module centred in the cell (centre at 3.75m) with 1.25m wall fillers on each side. The player-built stair piece is 5m wide, centred in its cell, and climbs the whole 7.5m cell. Kit stairs keep their module size: outside straight stairs run 5m at 45° and leave 2.5m of walkway before the foot; corner stairs end on the higher cell's edge. Stair rooms use a 2m landing and 2.16m flights and need no NavMesh links at the turns. A 2×2 tower is 15m × 15m. Breach ruins stretch to the 7.5m cell (cross-section across the full wall thickness) and each V half has a solid wedge collider under its slope, leaving the ground gap in the middle (about 2.25m) open for units. *SIEGE-18 (user, 2026-11-23)*: the Game ground is 32×40 cells of 7.5m (240×300m) with a 13-cell ring (97.5m outer, 82.5m courtyard; the northern approach stays 120m); the gate is the ring's middle cell on the north wall, 3.75m east of the ground centre line. Per-cell wall cost and HP are ×1.5 of the 5m-piece values. Siege ladder lanes stand every 2 cells (15m). The 3m prototype scenes are regenerated on the same 7.5m grid. Per-piece storey caps (`BuildPieceDefinition.maxStorey`): walls, bastions and stairs 3, the gate piece 1 (its column grows only by fixed walls), towers none (grid limit 4). The run-start castle is a one-storey ring of the shared wall piece with a two-storey gatehouse (gate plus one fixed wall) and three ground stairs from the courtyard. Run saves from the 3m grid (version 1) are no longer read.
- **Castle kit dressing in the Game (SIEGE-19, user decisions 2026-11-23; [history](../history/SIEGE/SIEGE-19.md))**:
  - The Game walls, bastions and gatehouse use the `PolygonFantasyKingdom` kit dressing proven in the SIEGE-14 spike, replacing the placeholder wall visuals. Kit dressing stays a visual layer: pieces that are under construction keep the placeholder and get no stair room until finished.
  - *Battlements (visual only for now)*: the kit crenels are visual only. `CastleBattlements` keeps the colliders, allied `ShotCover`, ladder sill and crenel stand points. Known mismatch until `SIEGE-20`: kit crenel sill 0.76m and merlon 1.38m against the rule values 1.1m and 2.2m; the Lv1 parapet also shows kit crenels.
  - *Convex outer corners*: each gets a small corbelled corner turret (visual only) at the top of the corner column, one per corner, placed at the shared vertex of the two outside faces. Raised corners keep one turret at the new top. A collapsed corner removes its turret.
  - *Gate columns*: a gate column (gate plus walls) raised to 3 storeys gets a stair room exactly like a wall column, from the lowest storey that meets a walkway (storey 2 beside one-storey walls). The gate storey itself is never a room. The raised portcullis (about 0.2m thick) hides inside the outer wall panel of the storey above the gate (0.5m thick), and the room interior starts inside the wall, so the stairs never meet it.
- **Phase restriction**: Construction/demolition/repair allowed only during Preparation; prohibited during battle (2026-11-11).
- **Vertical Layer Stacking** (confirmed 2026-11-11, Q-27 Option A):
  - 3m pieces stack vertically (up to 3 tiers in Game scene). Pointing at a piece stacks above it; empty cell places on ground.
  - *5m grid (Q-59, 2026-11-23)*: walls, bastions and stairs stay within storeys 1-3 (15m); only tower tiers may use storey 4 (20m). The automatic outside wall stairs connect only a one-storey wall to a two-storey wall; any step up to a three-storey wall (from one or two storeys) uses an interior stair room (below; revised by the user 2026-11-23, because an outside 45° flight takes a whole 5m cell of the lower walkway).
  - **Castle wall tiers (direction approved 2026-11-23, user; details and implementation `SIEGE-28`)**: a castle-wide wall tier upgrade raises, tier by tier, the wall storey cap and wall HP or the tower height, and unlocks wall structures; heights are counted in wall storeys (5m). The cap is only a maximum: raising existing walls stays a paid player action. Tower height follows the tier automatically for every tower (replacing "one storey above the touching walls"), so towers stay above the highest allowed wall; example: tier 1 walls 1 storey, tier 2 towers unlocked at 2 storeys, tier 3 walls 2, tier 4 towers 3, tier 5 walls 3, tier 6 towers 4 (to be tuned). The Lv2 battlement upgrade moves into the wall tiers. Decorations are not tier unlocks: the modular dressing adds them when its conditions are met, and interior stairs follow the wall structure.
  - **Interior stair rooms (Q-59, 2026-11-23, user; implementation `SIEGE-27`, spike first)**:
    - *Where* (revised by the user 2026-11-23): at every step up to a three-storey wall column, that is a one-storey or a two-storey wall beside a three-storey wall. Only the 1↔2 step keeps the outside automatic stairs. The room is a three-storey column's cell that touches the lower walkway; which of several joined cells get one is set by *Solid walls* below.
    - *Shape*: every storey of that cell from the lowest touching walkway up is a room with a floor: beside a one-storey walkway the rooms have floors at 5m and 10m; beside two-storey walkways only (2-3 or 2-3-2) there is one room with its floor at 10m and the storeys below stay solid. Each room storey has switchback stairs with a mid landing (two half-storey flights, about 45°, fitting the about 4m interior of a 5m cell); the top flight leads through a roof opening onto the three-storey walkway.
    - *Doors*: on every face that meets a walkway at the same floor height. Example: in a 1-3-2 row the room cell gets a door into its lower room on the one-storey side and a door into its upper room on the two-storey side.
    - *Solid walls, stair rooms only where the stairs are (user decision 2026-11-23, compromise option after reviewing how real curtain walls are built: two stone faces with a rubble core, climbed through towers)*: walls stay solid, and only the cells that carry stairs are rooms. In each group of joined three-storey wall cells, the cells that touch a lower walkway and sit at a row end (at most one neighbour in the group) are stair rooms; a group with no such end gets one stair room in its first touching cell (lowest x, then z). All other cells stay solid and the group is joined only along the top walkway. So four 15m walls side by side get stair rooms only in the two end cells. This replaces the short-lived open-hall version of the same day (neighbouring rooms opened into one hall).
    - *Stair layout (`SIEGE-27` implementation)*: every room storey of a stair cell stacks the same switchback, so the flight from below always arrives where the next flight starts (the stair foot). The two flights hug the two side walls, each about 2/3 of the old full half width (user, 2026-11-23), and the floor between them is a corridor from the foot to under the landing (2.3m headroom) on every storey. So the foot face and the opposite face can be entered on every room storey; the lowest room storey also takes a door on the second-flight side (under that flight); the first-flight side never has a door. Each stair cell picks the foot and first-flight faces that keep the most walkway doors (ties by face order); a walkway door that does not fit is left as plain wall (rare: walkways on perpendicular faces above the lowest room storey). The kit stair model's posts reach only down to the bottom of their flight, so a wooden post continues each upper-flight post to the floor. Temporary sizes (data asset `CastleKitSettings`): 45° half-storey flights 2.5m long and 1.46m wide, 1m landing, 0.31m wall inset, roof/floor opening over the second flight reaching 0.6m past its foot so the player's head clears it. On 7.5m cells (`SIEGE-35`): 2m landing and 2.16m flights (about 2.6m middle corridor); the turn NavMesh links are not needed there.
    - *Corner beside a stair room (user, 2026-11-23)*: a one-storey corner cell next to a two-storey wall whose only same-height way in is a stair-room door on its side (for example 2-storey, 1-storey corner, 3-storey) gets the usual quarter-turn outside stair starting on the door side. The door is centred on the wall while the stair's lower end sits against the wall edge, so this stair has no side boards in its collision and is boarded from its side next to the door; a NavMesh link joins the door floor and the lower flight. Steps up to a three-storey wall never get outside stairs, whether or not that cell is a stair room.
    - *Cost and look*: automatic and free, part of the wall dressing like the outside stairs; the cell keeps its HP and outer look apart from the doors; no arrow slits (those stay a tower feature). This improves the wall modular dressing, which may change for it. Three-storey walls will later get outer decorations such as banners (`ART-08`, after the storey work).
    - *Collapse*: follows the no-valley rule, so a collapsed room storey takes the storeys above it and the room disappears; if the lower neighbour falls, the door stays as an opening.
    - *Units*: player, allies and enemies on the walls can use the room (it is part of the NavMesh); whether enemies treat it as a target is `SIEGE-26`. The landing and the foot strip are narrower than two unit radii, so each turn also gets a two-way NavMesh link (`SIEGE-27`).
  - Stairs placed on tier-1 wall ascend to tier-2. Stairs can be placed before connecting to walls (shows warning prompt). On the 5m grid stairs stand on the ground or on a one- or two-storey wall, so their top never lands above storey 3 (Q-59). *Revised by the user 2026-11-23 (`SIEGE-27`)*: on the 5m grid the player-built stair piece goes on the ground only; stairs between walls come only from the automatic outside stairs (1↔2) and interior stair rooms (to three storeys).
  - **Collapse look (user, 2026-11-23; implementation `SIEGE-30`, spike first)**: a fallen wall storey leaves the V breach ruin from the SIEGE-14 spike. For breaches deeper than two storeys the spike offers two looks side by side behind a test switch, (a) the current single V piece stretched to the fallen height and (b) jagged end pieces stacked per storey with the V slope only in the top storey; the user picks one after viewing and the other is removed. A fallen tower or bastion leaves rubble plus a low jagged stump in its cells, and the wall faces it touched show torn-off scars (broken wall panels and cross-sections) instead of being re-dressed as plain outer faces (buttresses); scars are look only and do not change wall HP or movement. Building a new tower or bastion on that spot clears the stump and scars.
  - Lower piece destruction drops stacked pieces down 1 tier (retaining HP and mounted weapons); overlapping units fall and take height-based fall damage (temporary values for 5m storeys, SIEGE-18: no damage up to 1m, then 6 per metre, so one storey (5m) costs 24 as one 3m tier did, and three storeys (15m) cost 84). *Q-59 (2026-11-23)*: on the 5m Game grid this is replaced by the no-valley rule (`forbidValleys` on, [Decisions-Resolved](../plan/Decisions-Resolved.md)): a collapsed storey takes every storey above it, and the gap is rebuilt at the neighbours' height; settling stays only on 3m grids until `SIEGE-18`.
  - Detailed rules: No stacking on stairs; walls may stack above main gate; gate is fixed and undemolishable; when the gate breaks, only the lower door breaks while the indestructible stone arch, gate-top walkable surface, and any upper stacked pieces remain intact and walkable (no falling/collapse, new placement atop the intact gate/upper stack follows normal rules, and `Repair All` restores the door during preparation); multi-cell pieces require uniform support height and are destroyed if partially unsupported; no stacking on weapon-holding pieces and no weapons on covered pieces; no aerial cantilevering.
  - **Battlements (approved 2026-11-20, user; implementation SIEGE-10)**: Wall-top cover is placed automatically on the topmost tier of every wall column and rebuilt whenever pieces are stacked, removed or collapse.
    - *Two styles, one castle-wide upgrade*: Defense facility ("Prepare walls / weapons") Lv1 gives a **low parapet** (waist high: covers legs, upper body exposed). Upgrading to Lv2 replaces every wall top with **crenellated battlements** (merlons block arrows and sight; the crenel gaps between them are open). One style applies to the whole castle; no per-piece choice. The upgrade is a "Battlements" button in the build-mode panel under `Repair all` (build mode is entered with F at the courtyard `Construction` building; user decision 2026-11-20). The Defense facility has two levels (Lv2 upgrade 60 gold, temporary) and the upgrade is a free action (no prep clock).
    - *Temporary sizes (data asset, tune after playtest)*: thickness 0.4m inside the wall top (walkway 3m → 2.6m on one side), parapet 1.1m (raised from 0.8m by COMBAT-10, user 2026-11-21, for both Lv1 and Lv2: a crouched 2m unit, 1.1m tall, is fully covered while a standing one shows its upper body; the sill is also the crenel sill), merlons 2.2m tall × 0.75m with 0.75m crenels; each cell edge has a crenel in its middle and half crenels at both ends, so neighbouring cells continue the pattern.
    - *Blocking*: Physical colliders block enemy arrows and enemy line of sight. Defenders standing on that wall top shoot over or through their own battlement without being blocked.
    - *Aiming over cover* (user, 2026-11-20, after playtest: enemy archers stood facing defenders without shooting): shooters aim at the body centre first and, when it is hidden, at the upper body (`CombatProfile.upperAimRatio`, 0.85 of height = 1.7m for a 2m unit). The parapet (0.8m then, 1.1m since COMBAT-10) hides the 1m body centre from almost every ground position; the upper body (1.7m) is visible, so the parapet covers the lower body as intended. Merlons (2.2m) still hide a defender completely.
    - *Archer side-step* (user, 2026-11-20): an enemy archer with no visible target for 3s (`SiegeSettings.archerRepositionDelay`) tries spots beside its frontal approach point along the wall, 3m apart up to 3 steps each way (`archerSideStep`, `archerSideStepCount`), on the ground outside only, and walks to the first one from which any enemy in range is visible (body centre or upper body). This stops archers from standing still in front of a defender hidden behind a merlon. Anti-jitter (user, 2026-11-20): the blocked clock runs only while in range; one search per blocked episode, the next only after 10s (`archerSearchCooldown`); a found spot is kept at least 5s (`archerSpotCommitSeconds`) and afterwards only while still useful; with no spot the archer holds its ground aiming and waiting (for the ART-04 bow-hold pose) instead of walking left and right. Crouch-to-reload cover fire is COMBAT-10 (see [Combat](Combat.md)); character animations are ART-04.
    - *Durability*: Not destructible on its own; it exists and disappears together with the top wall piece it sits on. No separate HP or repair.
    - *Edges*: Only outward-facing edges of the castle get battlements. Edges joined to another wall at the same height, stair entries/tops and castle-inner edges stay open so wall-top walking is never blocked. *Inside* (user, 2026-11-22, `BUG-battlement-inner-faces`): the run-start (default) castle footprint (its ring cells and courtyard) is always inside, even when ring cells have fallen, so neither the courtyard side nor the wall ends facing a gap get battlements; elsewhere, outside is any empty cell reachable from the map edge. Faces are recomputed on preparation edits, save loads and when the preparation phase begins, never during battle, so a reload shows the same faces.
    - *Ladders* (revised 2026-11-20 after playtest): no gap. The pattern runs unbroken; ladders hang at the middle of a cell edge, which is always a crenel, and their top rests on the parapet/crenel sill (1.1m above the walkway since COMBAT-10). Climbers step over the sill onto the walkway. Without a battlement on that face the top stays at walkway height.
    - *Cannon gun ports* (user, 2026-11-20; narrowed by SIEGE-11 on 2026-11-22): at Lv2, the battlement face a siege weapon points at keeps only the sill (1.1m, no merlons), so shells do not visibly pass through stone; the bastion's other outer faces keep merlons and crenel firing slots. Merlons return when the weapon is removed. If the weapon faces an edge that has no battlement (for example toward the wall), nothing changes.
  - **Machicolations (decided by the user 2026-11-22; implementation SIEGE-12)**: Openings above the main gate that let defenders attack enemies hugging the gate base, which the footing line-of-sight rule hides from wall-top archers ([Combat](Combat.md) Line of sight).
    - *Where*: Only on the gate top for now, fixed and present from the start (part of the gate, free, never built or demolished). Machicolations on other wall pieces (a buildable piece or upgrade) are a later extension.
    - *Who*: Both allied AI units and the player. Standing at an opening, the attacker may shoot or drop straight down at the gate base through it.
    - *Attack methods*: Swappable, data-selected attack modules (same idea as `ISiegeWeaponModule`), so more methods can be added without changing the opening. SIEGE-12 ships only **arrows straight down** (existing arrows, damage and projectile rules; the opening simply does not block them). Dropped stones, boiling oil and similar methods are later modules.
    - *Method choice*: Automatic by attacker type, no selection UI: ranged units shoot arrows, and the player uses the method that matches the held weapon. Until another module exists, only ranged attackers (AI archers, the player with a bow) can use an opening.
    - *AI use*: An allied ranged unit in `Hold` near an opening walks to a free opening when an enemy is under it and it has no other visible target, shoots down, and returns to its post when no enemy is left below. One unit per opening, and not one someone else (the player included) is standing on. The walk must not drop below the post (same `HoldMaxDescent` rule as other Hold paths).
    - *Temporary values (data asset `MachicolationSettings`, tune after playtest)*: one opening per gate cell on each outward face, at the face middle (the crenel), so the 2-cell gate has 2; stand spot 0.85m inside the face, used within 0.6m; each opening's shaft covers its whole 3m cell width, from 1.2m inside the face (short of the door) to 2m outside, down to the ground; AI search radius 12m from the post. Placeholder look: an overhang slab on the outer face below the walkway and a dark hole marker on the walkway, with no collider and no NavMesh effect.
- **Bastions (approved 2026-11-22, user; implementation SIEGE-11)**: A dedicated buildable piece that juts out from the wall line and carries siege weapons (Bannerlord-style). In the Game it is currently the only weapon mount; the stronger tower below (SIEGE-14 spike) is the second one and joins the Game at integration. Ballistae and trebuchets are expected to replace the cannon later; until then the cannon uses bastions.
  - *Piece*: new kind `Bastion`, 1×1 cell, 3m tall (same as a wall piece), HP 150, cost 25 gold, one mount point on top. Temporary values in data assets; tune after playtest.
  - *Placement*: only outside the castle, against the wall. A ground bastion goes on an empty cell outside the castle (reachable from the grid edge through empty cells) that touches a wall piece on one of its four sides at the same tier and height. Bastions stack only on bastions, and each stacked tier must again touch a wall piece at that tier, so a bastion can match a taller wall. Nothing else stacks on a bastion and a bastion does not stack on other pieces. Chains of bastions without a wall beside them are not allowed. Agent defaults (2026-11-22), open to revision after playtest.
  - *Walkway connection warning* (user, 2026-11-22): a bastion whose top would not be level with any neighbouring wall top (for example a one-tier bastion beside a two-tier wall) is still allowed, but the build preview shows `보루가 아직 성벽 통로와 이어지지 않았습니다. 위에 보루를 더 쌓으세요` (like the stair connection hint). Such a top is cut off from the walkway until another bastion tier is stacked, so nobody can walk to a weapon placed there.
  - *Ladder lanes*: a bastion cannot occupy the cell where a ladder stands (the cell directly outward of a ladder slot). Ladders do not attach to bastions; dynamic ladder placement on bastion faces is left for a later task.
  - *Weapons only on outworks*: plain wall pieces no longer have a mount point; weapons go on bastions (and on towers once they reach the Game). Saved runs with a weapon on a plain wall drop that weapon when the save loads and refund its recorded value in full, following the META-10 removed-content rule.
  - *Firing arc*: because a bastion juts out, its weapon covers the wall face beside it: cannon auto-fire ±90° and manned traverse ±135° around the placement facing (were ±45° / ±90° on walls). Range stays 6–60m. Facing is still set with R at placement.
  - *Battlements*: outer faces of a bastion get battlements like any wall top (faces joined to the wall at the same height stay open, so the bastion top is part of the walkway). The gun port rule above applies to the face the weapon points at.
  - *Enemies*: Wall Breakers can target a bastion's bottom piece like any outer wall piece; its loss counts as a wall loss for morale. Enemies that reach the wall top and flyers attack the mounted weapon as before. NavMesh is rebuilt on placement like other pieces.
  - *Squad orders*: preparation squad destinations still use the wall and gate bounds, so a bastion top outside them is reached by the player only during preparation; squads fight there in battle normally.
- **Towers (approved 2026-11-23, user; implementation SIEGE-14 spike, Game integration later)**: A second, more expensive and stronger outwork beside the bastion, modelled on the PolygonFantasyKingdom demo tower. Bastions stay as they are; both carry siege weapons.
  - *Piece*: new kind `Tower`, 2×2 cells (10m × 10m on the 5m grid), built outside the wall line touching a wall like a bastion (outside cells, a same-level wall on its perimeter, not on ladder lanes). Placing it stacks tiers up to one storey above the tallest touching wall (capped at the grid's level limit; the Game grid allows 4 storeys so a tower always rises above 3-storey walls, Q-59; in the Game this placement rule is replaced by tower height from the castle wall tier, `SIEGE-28`). Only tower tiers stack on a tower, and a tower tier only stacks on a tower: no walls, stairs, bastions or gates on top. Temporary values: HP 400 per tier, cost 60 gold (Game only), four weapon mount points on the roof, 1.3m around its centre so cannons stay clear of the stair opening.
  - *Shape*: the top storey is a room with arrow slits on its outer faces and a straight interior stair up through a roof opening. *Revised by the user 2026-11-23 (`SIEGE-29`, because a tower taller than one storey above its walls had no door)*: every tower storey from the lowest touching walkway floor up is a room with an interior stair to the next storey, and each storey gets a door on every face that meets a walkway at its floor height; storeys below the lowest touching walkway stay solid with no stairs down (a 3-storey tower between two 2-storey walls has rooms only from 10m up). The stair layout reuses the `SIEGE-27` interior stair module. The roof has a corbel ring with battlements set slightly outward and taller corner merlons. The walkway is not blocked by any stair.
  - *Ranged buff*: allied ranged attackers standing on a tower roof get `AttackRange` +20% and `Attack` +20% (percent stat modifiers, removed when they leave). The values are data on the tower piece (`BuildPieceDefinition` occupant modifiers) and tunable; they stack with the high-ground range rule.
  - *Collapse*: the wall behind a fallen tower stays whole (same as a fallen bastion). *Revised 2026-11-23*: it leaves rubble, a jagged stump and torn-off scars on the touched wall faces (Collapse look above, `SIEGE-30`).
- **Build Controls**: 45-deg azimuth, 30-deg elevation orthographic camera; Q/E orbits 90 deg smoothly matching screen direction (Q left, E right); Left-click place, Right-click cancel, R rotate piece; translucent green/red preview.
- **Unit Overlap** (2026-11-11): Living units overlapping new placement are harmlessly lifted onto the new top surface.
- **Siege Weapons**: Mounted only on outwork mount points: bastions since SIEGE-11, plus the four roof points of a tower (SIEGE-14 spike; Game at integration). Decoupled from ground footprint. Cannons are one extensible type (`ISiegeWeaponModule`). Destroying supporting wall piece destroys mounted weapons simultaneously (no ground drop).
- **Cannon mechanics (approved 2026-11-13, Q-21; implementation SIEGE-02)**:
  - **Control**: Auto-fires at enemies in range when unmanned; the player can man it and aim/fire manually (more accurate than auto). Auto-fire pauses while manned.
  - **Shot**: Ballistic arc with area damage at impact; minimum range prevents hitting the wall base directly below.
  - **Aim arc**: 90° horizontal (±45°) around the facing set by R at placement; widened to ±90° (auto) / ±135° (manned) by SIEGE-11 bastions.
  - **Durability**: Own HP; enemies that reach the wall top and flyers can destroy it. Repaired by the existing `Repair All`. Still destroyed together with its supporting wall piece.
  - **Passage**: Does not block movement on the wall top; no NavMesh carve.
  - **Friendly fire**: None for now (allies and own walls take no damage), behind a data/rule switch so it can be enabled later.
  - **Auto targeting**: Nearest enemy in range/arc, implemented as a swappable target-selection strategy for later patterns (e.g., densest cluster, gate/ladder priority).
  - **Extensibility**: Behavior lives in `ISiegeWeaponModule` components configured by data; new weapon types reuse the shared body and mount rules.
  - **Temporary values (data asset, tune after playtest; user had no preference)**: Range 6–60m, blast radius 3m, center damage 40 (50% at edge), reload 6s (same when manned), HP 150, cost 30 gold (unchanged).
  - **Detail defaults (confirmed 2026-11-13)**: Man/leave with the interact key (F) near the cannon, also leave with Esc; mouse aims within the arc, left-click fires. Auto-fire impact scatters up to 2m (via `IRandomSource`); manual fire has no scatter. Enemies target a cannon only when no ally is within their reach.
  - **SIEGE-02 scope decisions (2026-11-13)**: R rotates a selected cannon in 4 directions (90° steps) at placement; facing is saved in the castle record. Cannon HP carries over between battles like walls (fixed by `Repair All`). SIEGE-02 also wires wall-top ladder climbers to attack a cannon when no ally is in reach (coordinate shared enemy AI edits with the enemy-assault owner); flyer targeting stays with B-6.3. Blast damage applies target defense (`DamageRules.Calculate`, minimum 1) after distance falloff.
  - **Usability (SIEGE-07, 2026-11-15; user delegated the choice to the agent, open to revision after playtest)**: (1) *Fire-ready feedback at the crosshair*: while manned the crosshair is green when the aimed point can be fired at, amber while reloading, red when the aim is out of range / outside the arc / on nothing; a reload bar and a one-line state (`Ready`, `Reloading 2.4s`, `Out of range`, `Outside arc`) sit directly under it, and the crosshair pulses once when reload completes. The old reload text stays in the battle status panel but was missed because it sits at the end of a long line away from where the player looks. Sound and on-cannon effects wait for the audio/art passes. (2) *The cannon follows the aim*: no rotate keys; the barrel swivels toward the view direction and snaps onto the target when a shot leaves. (3) *Manned traverse ±90°* around the placement facing (never backward into the castle); range stays 6–60m. The barrel turn speed (180°/s) is cosmetic and never delays a shot. Auto-fire keeps ±45° / 6–60m and the same targeting; its barrel also turns to each shot. The barrel returns to the placement facing when the battle ends. Values live in `CannonSettings` (`ManualAimHalfAngle`, `SwivelSpeed`) and `CannonOperatorSettings` (state colors, pulse).
- **Construction Economy** (2026-11-12, D-2): Spend run gold on walls/stairs/cannons. Demolishing refunds 100% for current-prep builds and partial percentage for past-prep/starting walls (gate non-demolishable). Single `Repair All` button spends gold proportional to missing HP (prioritizes heaviest damaged if funds low). Instant construction currently; delayed multi-stage construction planned later.
- **Preparation Gate Opening** (PREP-05, user approved 2026-11-15): During preparation the player opens and closes an intact gate with F in front of it so units left outside can return. Opening rebuilds the NavMesh without collapse events. When the battle starts, a held-open gate closes automatically and allies or the player standing in the doorway are moved inward. A destroyed gate stays open until repaired regardless of this toggle; repairing does not close a held-open gate.

## Enemies & Siege Strategy

- **Shared ladder traversal (approved 2026-11-13, Q-41; implementation SIEGE-04)**:
  - Allies and player may ascend and descend raised ladders. Current enemy-only ascent is an implementation limitation, not the shared traversal contract.
  - **Player controls**: Tap F at either endpoint to board; W/S moves up/down while aboard. Later confirmation on 2026-11-13: hold F at the upper endpoint to topple, rather than assigning a separate key. Boarding is triggered on release of a short press; a long press never boards on release. The short-press threshold is configured in `SiegeSettings` (temporary default 0.25s); raise/topple durations remain 3s/2s.
  - **Traffic**: One occupant total across both directions. Requests are served in arrival order; the player has no priority over units.
  - **Allied commands**: Automatically use a ladder when the route to the ordered destination requires it. A new order received during traversal takes effect after reaching an endpoint.
  - **Combat**: Occupants cannot attack, may receive damage, and fall if killed. Existing fall-damage rules apply when a ladder topples beneath a living occupant.
  - **Toppling safety**: Automatic allied toppling is prohibited while an ally/player is aboard or waiting to use the ladder. Enemy occupancy alone does not prohibit toppling. Existing nearby-enemy combat blocking remains; the player topples with the hold-F gesture. Deliberate friendly toppling was not approved; SIEGE-04 applies the friendly-use protection to both automatic and player pushes.
  - **Push feedback (user approved 2026-11-13, BUG-ladder-push-visual)**: Raising and pushing use continuous motion. Cancellation before the vertical balance point returns smoothly to the starting endpoint; once past balance, motion completes without further input, even if a nearby hostile arrives. Balance is derived from ladder geometry, not a fixed half-progress threshold. New boarding is blocked during push/settling; occupants pause climbing and follow ladder tilt while pushed or returning, resume once fully returned, and fall as soon as an outward push crosses vertical. Existing 3s raise / 2s push durations also determine settling speed. Support destruction and battle reset still force immediate logical collapse.
  - **Push weight & cooperation (user approved 2026-11-14, SIEGE-05)**: Supersedes the 2s push hold above; the auto-complete-after-balance rule stays. Push hold time = time from upright to the vertical balance point, 3s for one pusher on an empty ladder (`SiegeSettings.ladderPushHoldSeconds`). An occupant (ally or enemy; still one at a time) multiplies it by ×1.5 (`ladderOccupiedPushMultiplier`). Allies pushing together at the upper endpoint speed it up: 2 pushers +50%, 3 or more +75% cap (`ladderTwoPusherBonus`, `ladderThreePlusPusherBonus`); the player counts as one pusher. Post-balance settling and pre-balance release return keep the existing 2s full-rotation speed (`ladderPushSeconds`). Raising stays 3s. Existing friendly-use protection and nearby-enemy blocking are unchanged. Upper-exit holding (chokepoint standoff) and autonomous ally movement to nearby raised ladders are tracked in `SIEGE-14` (Q-60, 2026-11-22).
  - **Raising**: Allies automatically raise a lowered ladder when needed; the player holds F at the lower endpoint to raise it.
  - User confirmed shared-code edits are available for SIEGE-04 on 2026-11-13. The current implementation handles a ladder between two walkable endpoint regions; a complete walking route is preferred when already available. [Decision record](../history/Q/Q-41.md), [implementation verification](../history/SIEGE/SIEGE-04.md).

- **Siege Assault Rules** (2026-11-11, 2026-11-12):
  - Ground melee cannot strike wall-top defenders from ground level. Enemies enter via gaps, un-repaired segments, or broken gate.
  - Gate destruction (2026-11-14, `SIEGE-06`): Only the lower door breaks and opens the ground passage for allies and enemies; the surrounding stone arch is indestructible and cannot be targeted after the door breaks, while the gate-top surface and any upper stacked walls remain intact and walkable. Once the passage opens, `Gate Breaker` and `Wall Breaker` switch to `Engage` instead of continuing to strike the arch or upper pieces at the gate cell.
  - Wall-destroying enemies follow Q-43; B-6.2 implementation on `feature/b-6.2` adds wall/gate bottom-piece attacks and breach entry. Prototype prefab is available with default roster count 0; formal/mixed encounter integration remains B-6.4/B-7.1. [Verification](../history/B/B-6.2.md).
  - **Archetypes**: `Ladder Grunt` (enters breaches or raises/climbs ladders), `Gate Breaker` (enters breaches or attacks gate), `Archer` (shoots upward at 50% damage, keeps shooting from outside after a breach and enters only after a no-target delay, SIEGE-15; ally archers shoot downward at 100% and gain the high-ground range bonus, see [Combat](Combat.md)).

- **Additional enemy behaviors (approved 2026-11-13, Q-43 / B-6.1; implementation B-6.2~4)**:
  - **Extensibility (user requirement)**: Wall-target selection and flying movement/attack are swappable, data-selected modules (strategy/polymorphism), not hard-coded branches. Many flying enemy variants are planned; a new variant must be addable by combining existing modules plus data. Existing ladder/gate/archer behavior is kept, not re-implemented.
  - **Wall Breaker (ground)**: Target = nearest outer wall piece by the current selection module ("nearest" is the first module; others may be added later). The gate counts as a valid wall piece. In a stacked column it strikes the bottom piece; existing settle rules drop upper pieces one tier (on the 5m grid every storey above collapses with it, Q-59). Once a walkable path to any ally opens, it switches to normal assault (`Engage`) like other ground enemies.
  - **Flyer**: Flies over walls (not blocked by them; no ground NavMesh needed), then hovers at human height beside its target and attacks in melee. Target priority (updated 2026-11-23, SIEGE-16, see the bullet below): nearest ally atop walls, then mounted siege weapons, then nearest ally anywhere inside the castle.
  - **Flyer details (user, 2026-11-13, B-6.3)**: Hover height stays a per-variant data value so future flyers can either hover low enough for melee or stay high and require ranged fire. "Atop walls" = standing at least 1m above world ground (same as cannon targeting). Agent defaults pending review: no-target hold point is the castle-piece centroid moved 6m inward at cruise height (tallest piece + 3m); dead flyers drop without fall damage. [Implementation](../history/B/B-6.3.md).
  - **Flyer cannon targeting & hover slots (user, 2026-11-23, SIEGE-16)**: Target order is nearest ally atop walls → nearest mounted siege weapon (bastion tops and tower roof mounts alike) → nearest remaining ally. The literal "only when no ally is reachable" rule used by walking attackers would almost never fire for flyers (every living ally is reachable from the air), so the user chose this order. Flyers strike a cannon like wall-top climbers do (weapon size as reach, no defense, `BossUnit.StructureDamage`); ranged flyers deal the hit instantly without a visible projectile for now. A destroyed weapon triggers immediate re-selection. Flyers chasing the same target take different hover points at least `HoverSeparation` (default 1.2m, never less than the body diameter) apart, trying nearer points around the target first and then outer rings within attack range; candidate points are skipped when the flyer body would overlap structures (merlons, walls, corbels) or the target is not visible from there (`CombatPhysics.HasClearShotFrom`). If no point passes, it falls back to a crowded valid point, then to the old beside-the-approach point. All distances come from data and unit sizes, so the rules are grid-agnostic (5m kit). `FlyerSettings.TargetsSiegeWeapons` (default on) lets a variant opt out. *Roofed allies (user, 2026-11-23, SIEGE-16)*: an ally with a structure above its head within `FlyerSettings.RoofProbeDistance` (default 6m; interior stair rooms, tower interiors, gate passages) is not a flyer target and does not count as reachable for the retreat check, even when it stands 1m or more above ground; it becomes a target again as soon as it steps out from under the roof (next decision).
  - **Damage vs flyers**: The 50% upward-shot penalty does not apply when allies/player shoot flyers; they take 100%. The penalty remains only for enemies shooting up at wall tops.
  - **Ally melee vs flyers**: Allies attack a hovering flyer when it is within attack range, measured from its ground projection; they do not reach it while it travels at altitude.
  - **Failure handling (confirmed 2026-11-13)**: All roles re-decide every `AttackerDecisionInterval`; a dead or destroyed target triggers immediate re-selection. Wall Breaker with no reachable wall base holds and retries; Flyer with no valid target holds at altitude above the courtyard. Decisions pause during NavMesh rebuilds (existing rule).
  - **Ladders**: Pre-placed along wall perimeter (~every 5 cells on 3m grids, every 2 cells (15m) on the 7.5m Game grid per SIEGE-18; inactive if no wall piece at slot; attaches to topmost wall at cell). Raise ~3s, Climb ~2.5s (1 unit at a time, others queue at base), Topple: 3s hold to balance with weight/cooperation modifiers per SIEGE-05, then ~2s-rate settle (allies atop walls topple autonomously when clear of climbers, or player via interact key). Toppling drops climber with fall damage; toppled ladders can be re-erected in ~3s. On the 5m grid ladders must reach 5m, 10m and 15m walls (one, two and three storeys; Q-59, implementation `SIEGE-21`).
- **Siege AI improvement pass (Q-60, decided by the user 2026-11-22)**: Planned enemy and ladder-defense AI improvements are tracked in `SIEGE-14` (autonomous ally wall-top ladder push & upper-exit chokepoint defense), `SIEGE-15` (assault-transition thresholds to prevent single-ally kiting and courtyard archer rushes, plus target-relative archer positioning for flanks/bastions and `COMBAT-10` reload-crouch tolerance), `SIEGE-16` (flyer cannon targeting parity, hover slot separation, and ranged flyer sightlines), and `SIEGE-17` (enemy ladder NavMesh reachability checks and wall-breaker face approach spread).
  - **Assault transition (SIEGE-15, decided by the user 2026-11-23)**: A ground siege enemy standing outside the castle (ground level and not strictly inside the rectangle that holds every wall and gate cell; the rectangle is recounted only on preparation edits, so it stays fixed during battle and a castle with gaps at start still has an inside courtyard) switches to `Assault` only when (a) a walkable path reaches an ally that stands inside that rectangle (ring cells included) or 1m+ above the ground (wall top, bastion top) = a breach, or (b) a walkable ally is within `SiegeSettings.assaultLeashRadius` (8m) of it. A leash assault ends once no walkable ally is within `assaultLeashReleaseRadius` (12m) and there is no breach, and the enemy resumes its siege duty. Enemies already inside or on a wall top assault any walkable ally (unchanged). Agent defaults, open to revision after playtest.
  - **Archer support after a breach (SIEGE-15)**: Enemy archers keep their ranged positions outside after a breach and only fight walkable allies inside the leash. When a breach is open and the archer has had nothing to shoot (no visible target and no tolerated crouched target) for `archerBreachAdvanceDelay` (8s), it assaults through the breach and stays committed while the breach stays open, so a wave of archers cannot stall the battle.
  - **Archer positioning (SIEGE-15)**: The approach point is placed on the outward normal of the target's wall face: among the target cell's faces that open to the outside, the one most facing the archer (a bastion has three); with no such face (courtyard target), the direction from the castle centre to the target when it is on the archer's side, else straight from the target toward the archer. Side-step candidates run along that face. Reload crouch tolerance: when the last shootable defender ducks behind the sill (`COMBAT-10`) and stays in range, the archer keeps aiming in place for up to `archerCrouchTolerance` (4s) without starting the blocked clock or side-stepping.
  - **AI LOD (SIEGE-15)**: The siege decision interval is multiplied by the enemy's AI LOD tier (x1/x2/x4, `PERF-ai-lod`); NavMesh revision changes and ladder raises trigger an immediate re-decision, and a hit shortens the wait to the base interval. Retreat checks (`CanReachAnyEnemy`) use the same breach/leash rule, so a far sortie ally alone does not stop a wave that has lost its siege means from retreating. LOD2 archers skip side-step searches and test fewer defenders for a clear shot.
