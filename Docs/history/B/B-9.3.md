## Manual verification (user)

1. Open `Assets/_Project/Scenes/Title.unity` in the Editor and press Play. Click a save slot row and press `New Game` (if that slot has a run in progress, `New Game` needs a second click and discards it).
2. **Stage 1**: In preparation, break nothing on purpose. Hold `B` for 1 second to start. Fight with the player and squads (`F1`–`F4` select, `1` move, `2` attack, `3` hold). Topple at least one ladder (hold `F` at the top). Win.
   - Expected: reward screen appears once; `Claim Reward & Next Stage` moves to stage 2 preparation.
3. **Stage 2 preparation**: Expected: no enemy bodies or arrows from stage 1 remain in the field; ladders are down; wall damage from stage 1 is kept; fallen allies' bodies stay but they are out of their squads; squad morale shows the post-battle recovery.
4. **Stage 2 and stage 3**: Repeat. Let the gate break in one of them if you can. Expected: waves appear at their times (stage 2: 0s/40s/80s, stage 3: 0s/35s/70s), victory only after the last wave is cleared, and each next preparation is clean as in step 3.
5. **Defeat path**: In any stage, let the player die. Expected: result screen → `Return to Title` → a new run starts at stage 1 with a fresh castle and full enemy roster.
6. Optional standalone: run `Builds/Windows/ProjectNeo.exe` and play stage 1 to the reward screen.
- Reset: stop Play Mode. To restart from stage 1, press `New Game` again (second click discards the saved run) or use `Delete selected slot` (second click) on Title.
- Automated repeat: Window > General > Test Runner > PlayMode, filter `GameStageLoopPlayTests`, Run Selected.
