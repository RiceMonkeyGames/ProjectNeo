- Rule: The team delegated the formula to the agent's common-practice proposal. Run score (Q-70, chosen by the agent on team request, data values open to tuning): stage cleared 1,000 each; boss killed 500 each; enemy killed 10 each; main gate intact at a victory 200 per stage; at run end 100 per living hero, 10 per living grunt and 1 per 10 gold held; +5,000 for reaching the ending; points earned in post-ending loop k are multiplied by (1 + 0.5 x k). A defeat keeps the points earned so far. Shown on the ending and defeat result screens; it no longer feeds Commander EXP (Q-71).
