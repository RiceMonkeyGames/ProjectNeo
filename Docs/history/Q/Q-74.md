- Decided in the team meeting 2026-11-19 (requested and recorded by Hwang Seong-min), third agenda round.
- Options presented: kinds (A) 7 different, (B) 3-4 reused and scaled; roles siege / duelist / commander / artillery; special rules health bar / stagger resistance / morale effects; reward (A) gold and materials, (B) A plus a guaranteed equipment drop, (C) score only; timing (A) placeholder in M2 and real bosses in M4, (B) real bosses in M2. The team chose A, all roles, health bar only, A, A.
- Rule: Seven different bosses, one per stage. Roles to cover: siege (walls/gate), duelist (hunts the player and heroes), commander (buffs nearby enemies; killing it weakens the wave) and artillery (long-range fire at wall tops). Special rule: a dedicated boss health bar only (no extra stagger resistance or morale effects). Reward: extra gold and materials. M2 ships placeholder bosses built from scaled-up existing enemies; real bosses come in M4.
