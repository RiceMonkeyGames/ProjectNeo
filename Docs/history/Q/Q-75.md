# Q-75 | 2026-11-19 | Post-Ending Loop Rules

Last Updated: 2026-11-19 21:09:01 (KST)

> Purpose: Decision log. | Parent: [history/README](../README.md) | Related: [Decisions-Resolved](../../plan/Decisions-Resolved.md), [Backlog](../../Backlog.md)

- Decided in the team meeting 2026-11-19 (requested and recorded by Hwang Seong-min), third agenda round.
- Options presented: ending (A) choose Continue or End run, (B) continue automatically until defeat; difficulty (A) stat %, (B) more enemies/waves, (C) loop modifiers, (D) A + C; stages (A) same 7, (B) shortened; loop count (A) unlimited, (B) at most N; rewards same or growing. The team chose A, D, A, A, growing rewards.
- Rule: After the last stage an ending screen offers Continue (harder) or End run. Loops replay the same 7 stages, without a loop limit. Each loop raises enemy HP/attack by a data percentage and adds loop modifiers (e.g. faster enemies); rewards grow each loop. Values and the modifier list are proposed with FLOW-03.
