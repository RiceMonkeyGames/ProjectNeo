### 2026-11-13: Q-39 Post-battle morale recovery floor

- Assignee: youngho. Branch `feature/q-39`. Doc-only decision task; no code or Unity changes.
- Problem raised by user: With "+20 after each battle" and "routed squads restart at 80", a squad that held the line at low morale (e.g. 30 → 50) could start the next battle lower than a squad that routed (80).
- Decided with user: After each battle every squad's morale becomes max(current + 20, 80), capped at 200. The routed-squad floor is now a case of this rule (0 → 80) rather than a separate rule. No further decisions needed.
