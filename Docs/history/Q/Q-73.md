- Decided in the team meeting 2026-11-19 (requested and recorded by Hwang Seong-min), third agenda round.
- Options presented: promotion (A) automatic by EXP, (B) pay at a facility, (C) EXP then pay; place: tavern / barracks / new facility; start grade (A) hire tier, (B) grade 1; skills (A) 3 fixed per hero opened by grade, (B) random on hire, (C) pick 1 of 2 on promotion. The team chose C, new facility, A, A.
- Rule: A hero's grade rises when its battle EXP is full and the player pays a promotion cost at a new preparation facility (training ground; name, cost and EXP values in data). A hero starts at the grade of its hire tier (tavern tiers 60/120/200g). Each hero has 3 fixed skills in its data; each grade opens the next one (grade 1: 1 skill, 2: 2, 3: 3).
