# Q-76 | 2026-11-19 | Meta - Between-Run Unlock System

Last Updated: 2026-11-19 21:00:08 (KST)

> Purpose: Decision log. | Parent: [history/README](../README.md) | Related: [PrepEconomyMeta](../../design/PrepEconomyMeta.md#meta-progression), [Q-07](Q-07.md), [Decisions-Resolved](../../plan/Decisions-Resolved.md)

- Decided in the team meeting 2026-11-19 (requested and recorded by Hwang Seong-min) in three steps.
- Step 1: between-run rules follow Darkest Dungeon 2 instead of a gacha. Options presented: (A) one straight track per category, (B) branching tree, (C) A plus per-hero unlocks; refunds or none. The team accepted the recommendation: A, built on an entry + prerequisites model so B or C can be added later, no refunds, rising costs, old guaranteed-appearance flags become default unlocks. This superseded the Q-07 gacha pity rule.
- Step 2: unlock points are granted once at run end from progress (stage reached, bosses killed); the per-victory grant from C-4 is removed.
- Step 3: tracks chosen from ten candidates: 1 Heroes, 2 Equipment recipes, 3 Shop items, 9 Starting bonuses. Not chosen for now: facility max level, walls/cannons, work crews, prep hours, squads, morale.
- First entries and costs are data proposed with META-07.
- Doc-only change; no code, Unity or test run.
