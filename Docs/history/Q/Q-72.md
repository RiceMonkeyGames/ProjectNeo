# Q-72 | 2026-11-19 | Perk Direction & Skill Perks

Last Updated: 2026-11-19 21:09:01 (KST)

> Purpose: Decision log. | Parent: [history/README](../README.md) | Related: [Decisions-Resolved](../../plan/Decisions-Resolved.md), [Backlog](../../Backlog.md)

- Decided in the team meeting 2026-11-19 (requested and recorded by Hwang Seong-min), third agenda round.
- Options presented: structure (A) three category trees, (B) one large tree, (C) flat list; skill perks (A) in the Combat tree, (B) separate skill tree; learned skills on run end (A) reset with perks, (B) kept; more than 3 skills (A) equip 3 in preparation, (B) learn at most 3; points per level 1 / 2 / scaling. The team chose A, A, A, A, 1 point.
- Rule: Three perk trees by the existing categories: Combat (player), Economy (gold, items, preparation) and Unit Enhancement (squads, heroes). Skill-granting perks are entries in the Combat tree. Commander level-up gives 1 perk point. Skills learned through perks reset with the perk allocation when a run ends and are chosen again next run. When the player knows more than 3 skills, 3 are equipped during preparation.
