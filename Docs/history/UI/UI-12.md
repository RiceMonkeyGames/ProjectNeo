## Implementation

### Sidebar layout and setup menu

- New Editor `PrepHudSidebarLayout` (menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Preparation HUD - Sidebar Layout (UI-12)`, re-runnable, now the menu to use). It runs `PrepHudLayoutA.Apply` first, then rebuilds the header panel, squad panel, HP set, gold chip, enemy panel, key guide, hold ring and applies the 0.8 `HudScale`. Then `PrepHudSquadCardLayout` runs.
- `PrepHudLayoutA` (UI-06 menu) is kept but is no longer the menu to use.
- Header panel: dark plate in the thick gold `Box22` frame, `스테이지 N` title (size 21), `승천 N` on its right above 0, a gold divider, and the prep clock row (hourglass icon, `remaining/budget`, one gold segment per hour).
- Gold chip: dark plate in the thick gold `Box22` frame with a gold ingot icon, top-right, with the materials line. The enemy preview hangs right under it at the same width (260).
- Left panels (header and squads) are 260 wide, matching the right panels.
- Squad panel: uses the header plate and frame, ends just under the card column. The HP portrait and bar sit below it.
- HP set (portrait ring, `current/max`, segmented bar with one block per 10 HP, at most 20): kept at the same size, in battle at the same bottom-left spot without the panels.
- Key guide: no panel. Rows at the bottom-right, each a dark translucent key cap with a white action label. Order is fixed (interact, `F1~F10`, backtick select/deselect all, `부대 페이지`, squad move/attack/hold, window keys, battle start and wait (hold), menu, `H`). `H` folds it to one `H` row. The `HOLD` tag is removed.
- Hold ring: while exploring, holding `B` or `T` fills a ring around the crosshair (gold for battle start, blue for wait). Warnings show as one shadowed line at the bottom center only when needed.
- Scale: every preparation HUD piece except the HP set is drawn at 0.8 (`PrepHudSidebarLayout.HudScale`), each about its own screen anchor. The enemy preview gets the same scale in preparation only (`EnemyIntelHud.ConfigurePreviewScale`). Facility, equipment and organization windows and the battle HUD keep their size.
- Margins: panels sit 12 canvas units from the screen edges (`PrepHudSidebarLayout.Edge`); the HP set and the key guide bottom row sit about 8.7 from the edges (`HealthAt`, `KeyGuideBottom`).

### Squad cards (option A)

- New Editor `PrepHudSquadCardLayout` (run by `PrepHudSidebarLayout.Apply` after the squad panel). Card 70 tall.
  - Portrait box left empty (no squad-type icon); the squad number (its select key) sits on the top-left corner in a small gold ring.
  - Order (`정지` / `이동` / `공격`) as plain status text right of the name, colored (hold gray, move blue, attack red).
  - Headcount: one segment per member of the capacity (filled while alive), `alive/capacity명` on the right.
  - Morale gauge and line below (`사기 current/max`).
  - Selection outline surrounds the whole card with a faint gold wash.
- Runtime hooks: `PrepSquadCard.ConfigureDetails` / `ShowDetails` (number badge, order text color, headcount segments).

### Paging for many squads

- `HudStyle.SquadCardsPerPage` (default 5). The squad panel is exactly one page of cards plus a bottom row (message line on the left, page marks on the right).
- `[` and `]` (`SquadCommandSettings.PreviousPage` / `NextPage`) turn pages and wrap around. Selecting a squad on another page (`F7` and so on) jumps to its page.
- `PrepSquadCardsHud.ConfigurePages`, `NextPage` / `PreviousPage`, `Page` / `PageCount`. The label `[  1/2  ]` sits at the bottom-right of the panel; a red dot shows while a squad on another page has low morale. With one page neither shows.
- Key guide row `부대 페이지` with `[` and `]` as two separate key caps (`KeyHint.SecondKey`, `KeyGuideRow.ConfigureSecondKey`).
- Pure rules in `SquadCardPages`: page count, page of an index, `Step` with wrap, `Clamp`, `FollowSelection`, `AnyOutside`, `AtRisk` (low morale band), `Label`.

### Enemy panel

- Title `적 정보` (the battle title `남은 적 N` stays). One row per enemy kind (`EnemyIntelRowView`: empty portrait slot, name, `× count`); hidden groups as `?`, bosses as `보스: 이름`.
- The old multi-line text (`EnemyIntelHud.Body`) stays wired and shows only when there are no enemies.

### Title screens

- `FantasyHudStyle.FrameInset` (20 canvas units) on the gold tracery frame of every Title screen (main menu, slot window, expedition, unlocks), equal on all four sides.
- Corner links and labels (`뒤로`, `초기화`, `게임종료`, `보유 해금 포인트`) keep an equal gap to the frame (`CornerX`, `CornerY`, `BackLink`, `CornerLink`).

## Manual Verification Guide

1. Open `Assets/_Project/Scenes/Title.unity`, Game view 16:9, press Play, start a new game (or continue a run).
2. Preparation, first-person: the left column shows the header panel (title, `승천` if any, clock row with gold segments) and below it the squad panel with cards (number ring, order text, headcount segments, morale line). The HP set sits under the squad panel, bottom-left.
3. Top-right: gold chip with the ingot icon and gold number; `V` opens the enemy preview under it with `적 정보` and one row per enemy kind.
4. Bottom-right: the key guide with fixed rows, including `부대 페이지` with two caps `[` and `]`. Press `H`: the guide folds to one `H` row; press `H` again to unfold.
5. Hold `B` (gold ring) or `T` (blue ring) around the crosshair while exploring.
6. With more than 5 squads: `[` and `]` turn pages (`[  1/2  ]` label bottom-right of the squad panel). Press the number key of a squad on page 2: the page follows the selection. A red dot appears when a squad on another page has low morale.
7. Battle start: the HP set stays bottom-left without the panels.
8. Reset: stop Play Mode. Rebuild with `ProjectNeo/Setup/Preparation HUD - Sidebar Layout (UI-12)` (Editor idle; active scene saved).
