## Manual Verification Guide

1. Open `Assets/_Project/Scenes/Game.unity` and press Play, with the Game view at 16:9.
2. Expect the top bar, the squad cards, the HP panel (bottom right) and the bottom prompt line to have gold frames on dark parchment.
3. Press `F1`: squad 1 is selected. Its card turns brown with a bright frame.
4. Press `H`: the centered key list opens. Press `V`: the enemy preview opens at the top right. Both are framed.
5. Look at a facility: a framed context prompt appears under the crosshair (not yet verified by the agent).
6. Reset: stop Play Mode.

### Expedition screen (Phase 3)

1. Open `Assets/_Project/Scenes/Title.unity`, Game view 16:9, press Play.
2. `게임 시작` → pick a slot → `새 게임`: the expedition screen shows the backdrop, the `출정 준비` nameplate, the `난이도` and `해금` cards side by side, the parchment `출정` below and `뒤로` bottom-left.
3. On a slot with Ascension unlocked, click the gold arrows: `승천 N` changes within the unlocked range, the `승천 N까지 해금` line stays, rules and the multiplier update. On a fresh slot it shows `승천 0 - 기본 게임` and `아직 열린 승천 단계가 없습니다`.
4. The `해금` card shows `보유 해금 포인트 N` for that slot (same number as on the slot card). Click `해금 열기`, buy an unlock if possible, close it: the number updates.
5. `뒤로` returns to the slot window (header unchanged); `출정` starts the run in the Game scene.
6. Reset: stop Play Mode; reset test slots with `초기화`. To rebuild the look, run `ProjectNeo/Setup/Restyle Title Expedition Screen (UI-06)` with an idle Editor.

### Unlock screen (Phase 4)

1. In the expedition screen click `해금 열기`: a full-screen `해금` panel covers it, `보유 해금 포인트 N` top-right, `영웅` selected on the left with a gold frame.
2. Click each track button: the right side shows that track's cards (green 해금됨, blue 다음 후보, dark `X 먼저`), each with an empty framed thumbnail square on the left, and the `N/M 해금` counts. The `무작위 해금` button sits at the right edge under the cards.
3. With at least 1 point, click `무작위 해금 (1 포인트)` once: one 다음 후보 card turns green with a gold frame, the status says `X 해금!`, the points drop by 1. With 0 points the button is greyed out. A finished track shows `모두 해금함`.
4. `뒤로` returns to the expedition screen; its `보유 해금 포인트` matches. Back on the slot window the slot card shows the new points.
5. Reset: stop Play Mode; reset the slot with `초기화`. Rebuild with `ProjectNeo/Setup/Restyle Title Unlock Screen (UI-06)`.
