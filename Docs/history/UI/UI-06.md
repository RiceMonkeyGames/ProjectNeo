## Implementation

### Phase 1 (commit `14429c0`, on `develop`): preparation HUD structure

- Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Preparation HUD (UI-06)` (`PrepHudSetup`) builds the preparation HUD in `Game.unity`:
  - `PrepTopBar` (stage, PREP-06 clock moved to the center with a remaining-time gauge, gold and materials), driven by `PrepTopBarHud`.
  - `PrepSquadCards` column with the `PrepSquadCard` template (name, headcount gauge, morale gauge colored by band), driven by `PrepSquadCardsHud`.
  - `EnemyIntel` preview moved to the top right in preparation (left in battle) via `EnemyIntelHud.ConfigurePlacement`.
  - `KeyList` toggled by `H`, plus `KeyListHint` at the bottom right (`KeyListHud`).
  - `ContextPrompt` under the crosshair (`GameFlowHud.ConfigureContextPrompt`).
- Colors and sizes live in the `HudStyle` asset at `Assets/_Project/Data/UI/HudStyle.asset`.
- Pure text helpers in `PrepHudText` (EditMode `PrepHudTextTests`); PlayMode `PrepHudPlayTests`.

### Phase 2 (uncommitted): Fantasy Warrior look

- Panels: dark slate parchment fill `SPR_HUD_FantasyWarrior_Box_Small_Parchment02` tinted (0.13, 0.16, 0.17, 0.9). Selected squad card uses a warm brown fill (0.3, 0.22, 0.1, 0.94).
- Frames: gold overlay `SPR_FantasyWarrior_Frame_Box21_Variant01` (tint 0.78 grey, white when selected).
- Gauges: track `SPR_FantasyWarrior_Bar_Horizontal03_Mask`, with a uGUI `Mask` clipping a sliced fill `SPR_FantasyWarrior_Bar_Horizontal05`.
- Pixels-per-unit multipliers: panel 2, frame 8, gauge 6.
- Korean text keeps NotoSansKR. The pack fonts have no Hangul glyphs.
- `HudStyle` gained `panelPixelsPerUnit`, `frameSprite` / `frame` / `frameSelected` / `framePixelsPerUnit`, `gaugeFillSprite` and `gaugePixelsPerUnit`.
- New Editor `HudStyleLook` (menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/HUD Style - Fantasy Warrior Look (UI-06)`) writes those sprites and colors into `HudStyle.asset`. `PrepHudSetup` also applies them when it creates a new asset.
- `PrepHudSetup.Panel` adds a first-child `Frame` image (`LayoutElement.ignoreLayout`) and removes it when the style has no frame sprite. The `Track` and `Fill` helpers build masked gauges.
- `StyleSharedPanels` restyles the existing `InteractionPrompt`, `PlayerHealth` (panel, health track and fill; fill color kept) and `EnemyIntel` panels.
- `PrepSquadCard.ConfigureFrame` lets selection recolor the frame.
- Not restyled on purpose: Facility, Equipment and Organization panels (UI-07), and `BattleStatus` and the phase `Panel` (UI-08 / battle).

### Workflow to change the look

1. Edit `HudStyle.asset` directly, or rerun `ProjectNeo/Setup/HUD Style - Fantasy Warrior Look (UI-06)`.
2. Rerun `ProjectNeo/Setup/Preparation HUD (UI-06)` to apply the style to the scene.

## Manual Verification Guide

1. Open `Assets/_Project/Scenes/Game.unity` and press Play, with the Game view at 16:9.
2. Expect the top bar, the squad cards, the HP panel (bottom right) and the bottom prompt line to have gold frames on dark parchment.
3. Press `F1`: squad 1 is selected. Its card turns brown with a bright frame.
4. Press `H`: the centered key list opens. Press `V`: the enemy preview opens at the top right. Both are framed.
5. Look at a facility: a framed context prompt appears under the crosshair (not yet verified by the agent).
6. Reset: stop Play Mode.
