## Implementation

- `FantasyHudStyle` (Editor): shared helpers for Title screens in the pack's art. Sample sizes (3840x2160 canvas) are divided by 3 for the Title canvas (1280x720 reference); sliced sprites get a 3x pixels-per-unit multiplier. Provides the backdrop (painted background, tracery frame, curlicue gems), full-screen panel conversion, parchment (`Box_Parchment01` + `Banner07`), steel (`Banner08`) and caption-only buttons, and font assignment.
- Fonts are written through the serialized fields (`m_fontAsset`, `m_sharedMaterial`): right after a domain reload the TMP `font` setter on the dynamic Grenze asset (used while the main menu was English) sometimes kept LiberationSans and the scene was saved with it. Both setups check after saving that their labels kept the font and throw otherwise.
- `TitleMainMenuStyleSetup` (menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Restyle Title Main Menu (UI-10)`, re-runnable): `Panel` becomes full screen with `Visual` (backdrop, `Logo` = teal `Branding_Background01` plate in a `Banner13` frame with the dragon greebles, scaled 1.5x); `Label` = `프로젝트 네오` (Noto 46); `Button_Start` = parchment `게임 시작` with an arrow; `Button_Quit` = gold `게임종료` link in the bottom-right corner.
- `TitleSlotWindowStyleSetup` (menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Restyle Title Slot Window (UI-10)`, re-runnable): `SlotPanel` becomes full screen with the same backdrop and a header under `Label_SlotsHeader` (`슬롯 선택`) copied layer by layer from the sample nameplate `HUD_FantasyWarrior_Event_DialogueBox02/Name` (`Box_Background01` plate filling the nameplate, `Banner05` frame jutting out 55 / 33.6 / 28, text inset 80), built at sample size x 1/3 and scaled 1.6x as a whole. A first hand-assembled `Banner09` header left the plate visibly shorter than its frame and was rejected by the user. Each `Button_Slot N` is a 280x230 card: dark parchment fill (tint target), dim bronze `Frame_Box12`, gold `Frame_Box06` `Visual/Highlight`, static `Label_SlotName` and the detail `Label`. `Label_SaveStatus` sits under the cards; `Button_Continue` (parchment, `이어 하기`) and `Button_New Game` (the same parchment button, `새 게임`) below; `Button_Back` (`뒤로` with arrow) bottom-left; `Button_Delete slot` (`초기화`, red) bottom-right . Names and listeners are unchanged; it re-runs `ConfigureSlots`, `ConfigureSlotHighlights` and `ConfigureContinue`.
- `TitleScreen`: slot text is the card body, one item per line (`지휘관 Lv N`, `해금 포인트 N · 승천 N`, `최고 N`, then `스테이지 N 진행 중 (승천 N)` for a saved run; `비어 있음`, `손상됨 - 새로 시작`, `(백업에서 복구)`). The selected slot turns on its highlight (`slotHighlights`, `ConfigureSlotHighlights`, `IsSlotHighlighted`); scenes without highlights still get the old `> ` prefix. The continue caption no longer carries the stage. `StartGame` opens the expedition screen on the first click (the saved run is still discarded only by `BeginRun` on depart) and `DeleteSelectedSlot` deletes on the first click (`슬롯 N을(를) 초기화했습니다.`, `슬롯을 초기화하지 못했습니다.`, `빈 슬롯입니다.`); `IsNewGameArmed` / `IsDeleteArmed` were removed and `DeleteText` added.
- `TitleMenuSetup.SlotPanel` copies the Panel's Image/layout components only when present (the restyled Panel has none).
- A temporary `KoreanUiMigration` exception for the English captions was reverted when the screen went back to Korean.
- `KoreanFontSetup` now runs `ShaderUtilities.UpdateShaderRatios` on the font material before saving. Cause of the recurring `NotoSansKR SDF.asset` diff: the setup saved `_ScaleRatioA`/`_ScaleRatioC` at the default 1, and TMP rewrote them (0.8 / 0.65) the first time it drew Hangul in the Editor, so every test or Play session left the asset modified. The asset is committed with the computed values; glyph table and atlas are unchanged.

## Manual Verification Guide

1. Open `Assets/_Project/Scenes/Title.unity` and press Play (Game view 16:9, e.g. 1920x1080).
2. Main menu: dark blue backdrop with a thin gold frame, the teal `프로젝트 네오` plate with a stone dragon, a parchment `게임 시작 >` button, a small gold `게임종료` bottom-right. Hover both (they brighten).
3. Click `게임 시작`: the slot window shows the same backdrop, a steel `슬롯 선택` banner, three slot cards (last used slot has the gold frame), `이어 하기` and `새 게임` below, `뒤로` bottom-left, `초기화` bottom-right.
4. Click another card: the gold frame moves to it. A slot with a saved run shows `스테이지 N 진행 중` on its card and `이어 하기` is clickable; otherwise `이어 하기` is greyed out.
5. On a used slot click `초기화` once: the card shows `비어 있음` right away and the status line says `슬롯 N을(를) 초기화했습니다.`
6. Click `새 게임` (same parchment look as `이어 하기`): the expedition screen (old style) opens at once, even on a slot with a saved run; its `뒤로` returns to the slot window and that slot still shows `스테이지 N 진행 중`. `뒤로` on the slot window returns to the main menu. `게임종료` stops Play Mode.
7. Try a 4:3 and an ultrawide Game view: the frame follows the screen edges and the content stays centred.
8. Reset: stop Play Mode; reset test slots with `초기화`. To rebuild the look, run the two `ProjectNeo/Setup/Restyle Title ... (UI-10)` menus with an idle Editor.
