| Combat | `DamageRules`, `CombatPhysics` | Pure C# damage formulas, range, and line-of-sight checks. Attacks enemies in scene; allies do not occlude. | Combat | `CombatProjectile` | Ballistic projectile (COMBAT-09). `Launch(source, attack, launchVelocity)` stores damage, team and start velocity; each frame it follows the gravity arc (`CombatProfile.ProjectileGravity`), sweeps a sphere over the step and faces its velocity. Applies defense on hit. Passes through allies and the shooter's footing; stops at the first enemy, wall, ground or other obstacle, or when `ProjectileLifetime` runs out. On hit it calls `StuckArrow.Stick` when present, otherwise destroys itself. |
| Combat | `StuckArrow`, `StuckArrowLedger`, `StuckArrowSettings` (`ART-05`) | Arrows that stay stuck where they hit. `StuckArrow` on the projectile prefab: `Stick(hit, reach)` pushes the head `penetration` into the surface and anchors to the hit collider, or for a `CharacterActor` to the nearest humanoid/skinned bone (refuses actors with a camera child, the first-person player); `LateUpdate` follows the anchor without scale and destroys the arrow after `lifetime`, or when the anchor is destroyed or inactive. A static pure `StuckArrowLedger<T>` FIFO enforces `maxCount` (oldest removed first; EditMode `StuckArrowLedgerTests`, PlayMode `StuckArrowPlayTests`). | Combat | `CoverCrouchRules`, `CharacterActor.IsCrouched` / `CurrentHeight` / `CooldownRemaining` / `SetCrouched`, `CombatPhysics.IsBehindOwnCover`, `UnitCombatAI.UpdateCoverCrouch` (COMBAT-10) | Wall-top cover fire. Pure `CoverCrouchRules.ShouldCrouch` decides crouch: only AI-controlled allied ranged units with a battlement within `CombatProfile.CoverSearchRadius` (1.2m) crouch, mostly during the reload, and rise `CoverRiseLead` (0.5s) before the next shot; no target means standing. `SetCrouched` resizes the `CapsuleCollider` to `CrouchHeightRatio` (0.55) with feet fixed; `AimPoint` uses `CurrentHeight`, and the upper aim uses the target's `CurrentHeight`. Crouch resets on death, disable and ladder start. `IsBehindOwnCover` overlaps allied-passable `ShotCover`. [History](../history/COMBAT/COMBAT-10.md). |
| Combat | `UnitAISettings`, `UnitCombatAI` | Search radius, tick interval, rotation, movement, arrival thresholds. Picks closest reachable enemy, updates NavMesh path, melee/ranged attacks, stops on death. Empty-target Hold/Engage searches respect `DecisionInterval`; existing invalid targets drop immediately. Hold attack candidates use conservative collider AABB bounds before exact range/LoS; retained valid Engage targets reuse reachability path. Directives: `Engage`, `Hold` (melee advances within `HoldEngageRadius` 6m; ranged within attack range + `HoldRangedChaseExtra` 6m; return beyond `HoldReturnDistance` 1m; Hold chase/retaliation paths whose corners drop more than `HoldMaxDescent` 1.5m below the post are treated as unreachable via pure `HoldDescentRule.StaysAbove`), `Move`. Arrival: remaining <= `ArrivalDistance` (0.35m) or `MoveStallTracker` (`MoveStallProgress` 0.1m over `MoveStallSeconds` 0.5s within `MoveStallDistance` 1.5m). Move/Engage advance clears a rejected valid path and retries calculation/application once on the next AI update during NavMesh replacement; a second rejection or no route ends advance normally. New directives clear the retry flag. Default without orders: Engage. |
| Combat | `ShotBlock`, `RangedBlockAction`, `RangedOcclusionRules`, `CombatPhysics.ClassifyShot`, `UnitCombatAI.HandleBlockedShot` (COMBAT-12) | Ranged `Engage` occlusion. `ClassifyShot` checks the body-centre and upper-body lines with the same filters as `IsClear` (now `ClassifyLine(...) == Clear`) and reports `Clear` / `LivingEnemy` (with the nearest blocking enemy) / `Structure`; `RangedOcclusionRules.Combine` merges the two lines and `Decide` maps the result to `Attack`, `SwitchToBlocker` (blocker in range with a clear shot), `HoldDistance` (keep the range-based stopping distance) or `CloseIn` (structures: old stopping distance 0). Re-evaluated each decision interval; melee units keep the old stopping distance 0 when blocked. `UnitCombatAI.BlockAction` exposes the state. Tests: EditMode `EngagementRulesTests`, PlayMode `EngagementPlayTests`. |
| Combat | `MeleeRingRules`, `MeleeSlotBoard<T>`, `UnitCombatAI.ApplyApproachPath` / `TryPathToSlot` / `TryGetMeleeSlot` (COMBAT-12) | Melee approach slots for `Engage` and `Hold` chases (allies and enemies). Pure `MeleeRingRules`: inner ring radius = target radius + attacker radius + `UnitAISettings.MeleeSlotGap` (0.15m), clamped to 80% of attack range; outer (waiting) ring + `MeleeOuterRingOffset` (1m); `SlotCount` by `MeleeSlotSpacing` (0.9m, max 16); world-fixed slot angles (0 = +z, clockwise); `OrderByDistance`. Static `MeleeSlotBoard<UnitCombatAI>`: one slot per unit, one unit per slot, claims dropped lazily when the claimant no longer targets that unit or is dead/disabled (released on new directive, death, disable). Each decision tick a melee unit keeps its inner slot, otherwise tries up to 3 free slots per ring (nearest first; `NavMesh.SamplePosition` within `MeleeSlotSampleDistance` 0.5m, within `MeleeSlotLevelTolerance` 1m of the target's height, complete path, Hold descent rule), then falls back to the centre path. While walking to its slot the unit uses stopping distance 0 and does not stop just because the target is in reach (it still attacks on the way). `UnitAISettings.MeleeSlots` (default on) switches it off. |
| Combat | `UnitCombatAI.TargetDistanceScale` (`SIEGE-09`) | Optional per-target multiplier on the squared distance used to pick and switch targets (`FindTarget`, re-evaluation, retaliation).
