Castle wall art (`ART-06`, `feature/art-06`): `Walls/TiledBoxMesh` (unit box with castle-space metric UVs so coplanar overlapping parts show the same texels; one mesh per part, tracked in `Built.Meshes` and destroyed in `Discard`) is used by `CastleBattlements` when `BattlementSettings.textureTileMeters` > 0 (0 keeps the old builtin cube). Editor setup `Editor/CastleWallArtSetup` (menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Apply Castle Wall Art`, idempotent) replaces the `Visual` children of `Wall.prefab` and `Stairs.prefab` with Synty `PolygonFantasyKingdom` copies, removes model colliders (root colliders and NavMesh are unchanged), and sets the battlement stone material and 3.047m tile. Shared prefabs, so ConstructionPrototype, Siege prototypes and Game all change. Main gate stays a placeholder until `SIEGE-13`. Re-run the menu after regenerating these prefabs. [History](../history/ART/ART-06.md).

Auto battlements (`SIEGE-10`, `feature/siege-10`): pure `Walls/BattlementRules` (`CollectOutside` flood fill from the grid edge over empty columns; `Faces` bitmask N/E/S/W for a cell's top Wall/Gate piece — closed toward same-or-higher neighbours and non-outside cells; `Segments` for `BattlementStyle` Parapet=1 / Crenellated=2; a continuous sill plus merlons placed so every edge middle is a crenel) and `BattlementSettings` (asset `Data/GameFlow/Build/Battlements.asset`). `Walls/CastleBattlements` on the `ConstructionWorld` object rebuilds a `Battlement` child under each top piece on `ConstructionWorld.Changed`, on style change and when `IsUnderConstruction` flips (none while under construction); parts are `BoxCollider` + `Combat/ShotCover` (passable for `Allied`) with a `Visual` cube child, so they move and vanish with the piece and NavMesh treats them as walls. The outside set is recomputed only when `ConstructionWorld.LayoutRevision` changes (prep place/dismantle/restore) and when `Flow/GameBattlements` sees the phase enter Preparation (`CastleBattlements.RecomputeOutside`), so faces stay fixed during battle. `BattlementRules.CollectEnclosed` derives the start footprint from `ConstructionWorld.InitialPieces` (every cell the start layout keeps unreachable from the grid edge: ring cells and courtyard); the `CollectOutside(..., alwaysInside)` overload never floods into it, so a gapped default ring gets neither inner-face nor gap-end battlements (`BUG-battlement-inner-faces`). `SiegeLadder.EdgePoint` rises by `CastleBattlements.SillHeightAt(cell, outward)` so the ladder top rests on the crenel sill (no gap). Pieces with a mounted weapon (`MountLayout.HasWeaponOn`) use Parapet instead of Crenellated (`StyleFor`, `IsGunPortAt`) as gun ports. `CombatPhysics.TryGetShotPoint` (used by `HasClearShot` and by `CharacterActor` projectile aiming) tries `AimPoint`, then the upper body (`CombatProfile.UpperAimRatio`); `HasClearShotFrom(standPoint, ...)` checks a hypothetical spot. `SiegeAttackerBrain.DecideArcher` side-steps through the pure `Siege/ArcherCoverPlan` (in-range blocked delay, one search per cooldown, spot commit, wait in place; EditMode `ArcherCoverPlanTests`) with `TryFindFiringPosition` / `CanShootFrom`, and exposes `IsArcherFiring` / `IsArcherRepositioning` / `IsArcherWaiting` for animation. `ShotCover.Passes` is checked by `CombatPhysics.HasClearShot`, `CombatProjectile`, `CannonShell` and the `CannonOperator` aim ray. `Flow/GameBattlements` applies `FacilityLevel.battlementStyle` of the Defense facility (Lv1 Parapet, Lv2 Crenellated) every frame and drives the build-panel `Button_Battlements` (`TryUpgrade`: preparation only, `RunState.TryUpgradeFacility`); the Defense facility window text (`GameFacilities.DescribeDefense`) is ready but the facility still has no station of its own. Setup menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Add Battlements To Game Scene` (`Editor/BattlementSetup`). Game scene only; prototype scenes have none. COMBAT-08: `Walls/BattlementRules.WalkwayWidth` (wall-top walkway width along an axis-aligned direction within 30 degrees, wall top only, minus battlement thickness on faces that have battlements) and `CastleBattlements.WalkwayWidthAt` feed the squad drag-line cap (`SquadCommandCenter.WalkwayWidth`, wired by `GamePrepPlacement`). COMBAT-10: `BattlementRules.CrenelCenters` (pure crenel centres along an edge, Crenellated style) and `CastleBattlements.CollectCrenelStandPoints` (stand points 0.45m inside each crenel; skips gun ports, pieces under construction and corner end crenels) feed `CrenelSlotRules` through `GamePrepPlacement`. Parapet sill raised to 1.1m for Lv1 and Lv2 (`BattlementSettings.parapetHeight`). [History](../history/COMBAT/COMBAT-10.md).

Gate door-only destruction (`SIEGE-06`, `feature/siege-06`): `Editor/ConstructionGateArchMesh` generates `Art/Placeholder/ConstructionGateArch.asset`, and `MainGate.prefab` splits `Visual/StoneArch` (`ConstructionStone.mat`) + three arch `BoxCollider`s from `Visual/Door` (`ConstructionGate.mat`) + `doorCollider` wired to `PlacedStructure`. On gate `Health.Died`, `ConstructionWorld.BreachGate` disables only the door collider/visual, fires `PieceCollapsing`, and triggers a NavMesh rebuild without removing the gate from `BuildLayout` or settling upper pieces. `Health.ReviveFull`, `GameBuildEconomy.RepairAll`, and `ConstructionWorld.NotifyGateRepaired` restore the door in preparation. `GameBattleController.GateDestroyed`, `SiegeDirector.GateDestroyed`, `GameSquadMorale`, and `SiegeAttackerBrain` treat `Health.IsDead` gates as breached and untargetable. [History](../history/SIEGE/SIEGE-06.md).

Mixed enemy composition (`B-6.4`, `feature/b-6.4`): `Editor/EnemyCompositionVerificationSetup` creates `Scenes/EnemyCompositionVerification.unity` with a separate 5-enemy roster (2 ladder, 1 wall breaker, 1 melee flyer, 1 high projectile flyer). The high variant combines existing flight modules, higher hover data and the existing archer profile/definition; no additional runtime behavior class. `SiegeDirector` passes scene settings into `SiegeAttacker.Bind` for both ground/flying spawns. Four `EnemyCompositionPlayTests` exercise mixed closed-wall behavior, breach entry, data-only variant and roster/start/death accounting. Game's formal encounters remain B-7.1. [Verification and reset](../history/B/B-6.4.md).

Shared ladders (`SIEGE-04`, `feature/siege-04`): pure `Siege/LadderTraffic<T>` enforces FIFO single occupancy for both directions and teams. `SiegeLadder` keeps legacy enemy raising/climbing APIs while routing their occupancy through the shared actor queue; it also handles allied raising and friendly-use automatic-topple protection. `Siege/LadderTraveller` is added at runtime by allied `UnitCombatAI` or `PlayerInputController`, without prefab/scene edits. It selects a reachable ladder route only when the ordered destination lacks a complete walking path, drives approach/ascent/descent, defers orders received aboard, and restores NavMeshAgent/CharacterController or hands interruption/death to `UnitFallController`. Player uses tap F boarding, W/S climbing and hold F raising/toppling; `SiegeSettings.LadderTapSeconds` controls the temporary 0.25s tap threshold. `CharacterActor.IsOnLadder` prevents new attacks and cancels pending impacts while remaining damageable. GameFlowHud/SiegeStatusHud show contextual traversal hints. [Verification](../history/SIEGE/SIEGE-04.md).

One-cell stairs (`SIEGE-03`): shared `Data/ConstructionPrototype/Stairs.asset` uses a 1×1 footprint and 3m height. `ConstructionWorld.SetPose` scales the existing normalized ramp collider and Visual to 3×3×3m; no mesh or material change is needed for size. The ramp mesh (`Art/Placeholder/ConstructionRamp.asset`) is generated by `Editor/ConstructionRampMesh` with per-face vertices for flat shading (`ART-01`; menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Flatten Stair Shading` rewrites it in place). `Editor/OneCellStairsMigration` retains high landings while moving initial seeds in ConstructionPrototype, WallCommandPrototype, SiegePrototype and Game (7 stairs total); repeated execution does not move them again. Setup generators now emit the new locations. Humanoid NavMesh maximum slope is 46° to admit the exact 45° ramp; `Characters/PlayerControlSettings.MaximumWalkSlope` defaults to 46° and is applied by PlayerInputController.Awake. Four Game PlayMode cases cover both traversal directions, rotations, two tiers and disconnection after stair destruction. [History](../history/SIEGE/SIEGE-03.md).

Construction wheel input (`BUG-build-camera-zoom`): ConstructionCameraController uses normalized scroll steps directly; only Windows editor/player with KeepPlatformSpecificInputRange divides by 120. GameFlowPlayTests.ConstructionWheelInput_UsesNormalizedStepsAndRestoresCamera covers actual input → Update → zoom/reset; user confirmed physical wheel zoom works (2026-11-13). [History](../history/BUG/BUG-build-camera-zoom.md).

Grid display (`BUG-build-grid-lines`): BuildCameraSettings.MinimumGridLinePixels (default 1.5px) is exposed through ConstructionCameraController. BuildGridView updates its cached mesh before each URP Game camera render, computing perpendicular pixel widths separately for the two grid axes; original world line width remains the minimum. Cell centers/spacing, occupancy and picking stay unchanged. Cameras without an orthographic construction controller use original width. [History](../history/BUG/BUG-build-grid-lines.md).

Ladder push feedback (`BUG-ladder-push-visual`): `SiegeLadderState.RaisedFraction` drives the visual in both directions. `SiegeLadder.HasWall` derives `BalancePoint` from the angle between the outward horizontal and the actual base-to-edge vector (vertical / full raised angle). Before balance, cancellation settles back; after balance, raising/pushing auto-completes using existing duration-based rotation rates. `IsSettling` blocks new raise/entry requests, and `CanBoard` also blocks entry during controlled pushing. `LadderTraveller` shows a motion hint and prevents release-tap boarding during motion. The visual and occupants share `CurrentDirection` / `FollowTilt`; `SiegeAttackerBrain` and `LadderTraveller` pause traversal while PushProgress is positive (including return), preserve their attachment point and resume after return. FollowTilt moves only the projected rail contact point and keeps the body/approach offset in world space, preventing low-height boarding offsets from rotating below ground. `ClimberDislodged` releases occupants at the outward vertical balance point, separately from the later `Toppled` endpoint event. The event observes the exact balance pose even on a long tick. Existing fall controllers start there; support collapse/reset still forces immediate collapse. No serialized asset edits. [Verification](../history/BUG/BUG-ladder-push-visual.md).

Ladder fall separation (`BUG-ladder-fall-clipping`, `fix/bug-ladder-fall-clipping`): `SiegeSettings.LadderFallClearance` (`0.55f`) and `LadderFallOutwardSpeed` (`3.5f`) drive `SiegeLadder.ComputeFallSeparation` / `TryGetFallMotion`, and `UnitFallController.BeginFall(..., fallHorizontalVelocity)` applies obstacle-checked outward horizontal motion during `SiegeAttackerBrain.Drop` and `LadderTraveller.Drop` while preserving fall height and damage rules. [Verification](../history/BUG/BUG-ladder-fall-clipping.md).
