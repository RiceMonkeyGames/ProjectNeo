## Editor Scripts (`Assets/_Project/Scripts/Editor/`)

- `TitleMenuSetup.cs` (`UI-05`): Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Split Title Main Menu And Slot Window (UI-05)`. Splits `Title.unity` into the main menu (`Panel`: logo, `Button_Start`, `Button_Quit`) and `SlotPanel` (slots, status, continue, new game, delete, `Button_Back`), turns the Ascension window into the expedition screen (header, `Button_Unlocks`, depart, back) and draws `UnlockOverlay` above it. Keeps object names and references. Re-runnable.
- `PrepHudSetup.cs` (`UI-06`): Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Preparation HUD (UI-06)`. Builds the preparation HUD in `Game.unity`: `PrepTopBar`, `PrepSquadCards` (`PrepSquadCard` template), top-right `EnemyIntel` placement in preparation, `KeyList` / `KeyListHint` and the framed `ContextPrompt`. Frames and gauges come from `HudStyle`; `StyleSharedPanels` restyles `InteractionPrompt`, `PlayerHealth` and `EnemyIntel`. Tests: EditMode `PrepHudTextTests`; PlayMode `PrepHudPlayTests`. Re-runnable.
- `PrepHudLayoutA.cs` (`UI-06`): Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Preparation HUD - Layout A (UI-06)`. Runs `PrepHudSetup.Apply`, then rebuilds the preparation HUD art after the pack sample `06_Demo_FantasyWarrior_HUD_Adventure01` under `Visual` children (stage banner and `StageTitle`, compass-style `PrepClock` ribbon, `ResourceChip` with coin and `Gold`, portrait squad cards with `SPR_SelectOutline`, sample 02 health bar in `PlayerHealth/Box`, battle-start plate with `HoldGauge`), places `EnemyIntel` and `KeyList` on the right, and creates `Art/Fonts/NotoSansKR SDF - HUD Outline.mat` (drop-shadow preset for text over the 3D view). Wires `PrepTopBarHud.ConfigureTitle` / `ConfigureGold`, `PrepSquadCard.ConfigureSelectionOutline` and `GameFlowHud.ConfigureHoldGauge`. Re-runnable; this is the menu to use for the preparation HUD now.
- `HudStyleLook.cs` (`UI-06`): Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/HUD Style - Fantasy Warrior Look (UI-06)`. Writes the Fantasy Warrior panel, frame and gauge sprites, colors and pixels-per-unit into `Data/UI/HudStyle.asset`. Re-runnable; rerun the Preparation HUD menu afterwards.
- `KoreanFontSetup.cs` (`UI-04`): Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Korean Font`. Creates `Art/Fonts/NotoSansKR SDF.asset` from `NotoSansKR-Regular.otf`, pre-fills the glyphs in `KoreanCharset.txt` and registers it in the global TMP fallback list. Re-runnable.
- `KoreanUiMigration.cs` / `KoreanUiMigration.Texts.cs` (`UI-04`): Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Migrate UI To Korean`. Rewrites English scene/prefab TMP captions and definition display strings to Korean through Editor APIs from the `Texts` table; `Audit()` lists unmapped English text (Korean strings with key names or units also show up and are fine), `Dump(path)` writes all of it, `ReplaceInDisplayText(from, to)` fixes a glossary term (UI-09). Use after merging work that adds English captions. `EnglishUiMigration.cs` (UI-02) is obsolete.
- `EnemyIntelHudSetup.cs` (`C-5`): Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Add Enemy Intel HUD (C-5)`. Adds/updates the left `EnemyIntel` panel (just below `SquadCommandHUD/SquadsBacking`, `(16, -272)` at 1280×720 since PREP-09) (title + rows, height fits content) and `EnemyIntelHud` on `GameFlowHUD` in `Game.unity`. Re-runnable.
- `PrepTimeSetup.cs` (`PREP-06`): Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Configure Preparation Time (PREP-06)`. Creates `Data/GameFlow/PrepTime.asset` if missing, links it to `RunStart.asset` when empty, and adds/updates the top-center `PrepClock` line and `PrepClockHud` on `GameFlowHUD` in `Game.unity` (keeps the phase box at its original position). Re-runnable.
- `EquipmentUpgradeSetup.cs`: `ProjectNeo/Setup/Configure Equipment Upgrades` creates the shared settings, binds RunStart and fills only empty item upgrade-material lists from crafting materials × target level; keeps existing data and does not edit the scene. Also called by the facility generator.

- `RunSaveSetup.cs` (`FLOW-02`): Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Refresh Run Save Content (FLOW-02)`. Rebuilds `Data/GameFlow/RunContent.asset` from every definition asset under `Assets/_Project` (items, materials, heroes, soldiers, run-start facilities, castle pieces with asset GUID ids, siege weapons), logs id problems, and links it into `Battle.asset`; no scene edit. Re-run after adding or deleting definitions. `GameFlowSetup.ApplyTitleSaveSlots` also creates the Title `Button_Continue`. Tests: EditMode `RunSaveTests` (14, includes a stale-catalog guard); PlayMode `GameRunSavePlayTests` (5, Title → Game with a temporary save folder).
- `StageSetup.cs` (`B-7.1`, `CONTENT-01`): Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Create Stage Data (B-7.1)`. Idempotently creates `Data/GameFlow/Stages/` spawn points (Left/Center/Right), `Stage01–07.asset`, `StageCatalog.asset`, appends catalog rows for stages missing from an existing catalog (existing rows and order kept), and links the catalog into `Battle.asset` only when empty; keeps existing assets; no scene edit. Tests: EditMode `StageWaveTests` (13: timetable, replaceable start rule, retreat switch, plan building, catalog); EditMode `StageCatalogDataTests` (2, real assets: 7 stages in order and wired into `Battle.asset`; 2-3 waves, rising start times, complete groups); PlayMode `GameWavePlayTests` (4, Game scene: timed single release, pending blocks victory, retreat switch, real Stage 1 asset). `SingleWaveEncounterFixture` (PlayMode `[SetUpFixture]`) makes all other PlayMode suites use the old single enemy list via `GameBattleController.EncounterSourceOverride`.
- `BossSetup.cs` (`SIEGE-09`): Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Create Stage Bosses (SIEGE-09)`. Idempotently creates the seven placeholder bosses in `Data/GameFlow/Stages/Bosses/` (`boss_gate_crusher`, `boss_duelist`, `boss_marksman`, `boss_war_captain`, `boss_siege_giant`, `boss_sky_hunter`, `boss_warlord`) and puts each into the last wave of `Stage01-07.asset` at the center spawn point. Existing boss assets and waves that already hold a boss are left as they are.
- `CombatPrototypeSetup.cs`: Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Create Combat Prototype`. Generates B-2 scene, prefabs, and assets.
- `UnitBattleSetup.cs`: Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Create Unit Battle Prototype`. Generates B-3 scene, prefabs, assets, and NavMesh.
- `ConstructionPrototypeSetup.cs`: Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Create Construction Prototype`. Generates B-4 construction scene and assets.
- `SiegeWeaponSetup.cs`: Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Add Siege Weapons To Construction Prototype`. Adds cannon assets and buttons. Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Apply Cannon Health (Q-21)` sets `Cannon.asset` HP to 150 (idempotent).
- `CannonSetup.cs` (`SIEGE-02`): Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Connect Cannon Firing (SIEGE-02)`. Idempotently creates `Data/ConstructionPrototype/CannonSettings.asset`, `Prefabs/ConstructionPrototype/CannonShell.prefab` (placeholder ball + blast view), adds/configures `CannonModule` on `Cannon.prefab`, creates `Data/GameFlow/CannonOperator.asset`, and wires `CannonOperator` on the Game player and `GameFlowHud`. Tests: EditMode `CannonRulesTests`; PlayMode `CannonPlayTests` (ConstructionPrototype), `CannonAttackPlayTests` (SiegePrototype), `GameCannonPlayTests` (Game).
- `WallBreakerSetup.cs`: Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Add Wall Breaker Assets`. Idempotently creates target-strategy/settings assets and an inactive wall-breaker prefab, ensures SiegePrototype grid supports at least three tiers, then registers the prefab in its roster at count 0. Existing positive counts/configuration are retained.
- `SquadCommandSetup.cs`: Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Create Squad Command Prototype`. Generates B-5 scene and HUD (`Upgrade()` adds team vision and markers).
- `WallCommandSetup.cs`: Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Create Wall Command Prototype`. Creates B-5.4 integrated scene.
- `FirstPersonSetup.cs`: Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Upgrade Player Views To First Person`. Converts scenes to 1st-person cameras.
- `GameFlowSetup.cs`: Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Create Game Flow Scenes` creates `Title` and `Game` scenes (`AddCastle()`, `ApplyDefaultCastle()` for 80×100 grid, camera, default wall layout).
- `GameBattleSetup.cs`: Connects siege battle and gate banner interaction in Game scene.
- `GameRewardSetup.cs`: Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Connect Rewards To Game Scene`. Generates materials, items, and reward controllers.
- `GameBuildEconomySetup.cs`: Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Connect Build Costs To Game Scene`. Connects build costs, demolition, and repairs.
- `GameFacilitySetup.cs`: Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Connect Facilities To Game Scene`. Generates 5 facility definitions, `GameFacilities`, `FacilityStation` on 4 courtyard buildings, `FacilityPanel`, D-5 `Economy.asset` (50% sell), 4 shop items (`Data/GameFlow/Rewards/item_*`), level stock pools, and D-7 `EquipmentPanel` / `GameEquipment` / `I` key.
- `SquadCapsSetup.cs` (`PREP-09`): Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Apply Final Squad Caps (PREP-09)`. Creates Game-only `Data/GameFlow/SquadConfig.asset` (10 squads x 20 soldiers, captain not counted) and `Data/GameFlow/Command.asset` (WallCommand formation, select keys `F1`-`F10`), links them to the Game `SquadCommandCenter` (prototype assets untouched), grows the squad list to `GameBattleSetup.SquadListHeight`, moves `EnemyIntel`, and applies `SquadOrganizationSetup.ApplyScrolling`. Re-runnable. Tests: PlayMode `GameSquadCapsPlayTests` (2).
- `SquadOrganizationSetup.cs`: Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Connect Squad Organization To Game Scene`. D-8 `OrganizationPanel` (UI-01: card scroll, PREP-09 `ApplyScrolling`: both directions with a horizontal bar, cards sized to their rows by `GameSquadOrganization`, card template with Header button + List, row template) (reuses `GameFacilitySetup` popup helpers) and `GameSquadOrganization` on the battle object; marks `Interaction.asset` dirty to save the new `O` key.
- `TavernSetup.cs`: Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Create Tavern Heroes`. D-3 hero assets in `Data/GameFlow/Heroes/` (swordsman/archer tier 1 60g, knight tier 2 120g, paladin tier 3 200g; grunt prefabs as bodies) and `Facility_Tavern.asset` level hero pools (only when empty). No scene edits.
- `GameUnitSpawnSetup.cs`: Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Connect Unit Spawning To Game Scene`. Wires `GameUnitSpawner` and fills soldier prefab slots.
- `BarracksSetup.cs`: Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Configure Barracks Data`. Configures `Facility_TrainingGround.asset` and `allied_melee`/`allied_ranged` recruit costs/names.
- `SiegePrototypeSetup.cs`: Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Create Siege Prototype`. Clones siege prototype scene.
- `CharacterModelSetup.cs` (`ART-03`, `ART-04`): Menu `ProjectNeo/Setup/Apply Character Models`. Puts Synty models and hand weapons under unit prefab `Visual`, converts materials to URP copies, regenerates the four `AC_Unit_*` controllers, `AM_UpperBody` mask and `CAP_Unit_*` animation profiles (measures melee weapon contact from the right-hand speed peak), and configures `CharacterModelAnimator`. Re-runnable; rerun after any menu that regenerates unit prefabs. Details: [PrototypeScenes](PrototypeScenes.md).
- `BuildScript.cs`: Windows standalone build script (`Builds/Windows/ProjectNeo.exe`).

## PlayMode Tests (`Assets/Tests/PlayMode/`)

- `GameConstructionWorkPlayTests.cs` (`SIEGE-08`, `Q-77`): Game scene work orders: battle-start warning text (in progress / not started / empty after battle start), under-construction view and stacking/mount refusal, two crews, battle-start cleanup (0% removed and refunded, HP = progress), unfinished cannon not armed until fully repaired, repair refunds and gate closing on completion, preparation save records.
- `GameEnemyIntelPlayTests.cs` (`C-5`): Game scene preview closed until toggled, `? × N` for a hidden group, always shown in battle with live remaining counts after kills, hidden group revealed when its wave is released, no overlap with `SquadsBacking` and `BattleStatus`, hidden on reward, hidden again next preparation.
- `GameBossPlayTests.cs` (`SIEGE-09`): Game scene boss released with its wave (inactive before, Visual scaled, boss HP/ATK, preview row), boss health bar shown/updated/hidden, commander aura buff and wave penalty after death, boss gold/material in the victory reward and `ScoreTally.BossesKilled`; an artillery boss stays (range 22) while a regular archer retreats, and victory waits for the boss.
- `GamePrepTimePlayTests.cs` (`PREP-06`): Game scene clock from the real asset, top-center line clear of facility/equipment windows and hidden over the organization window, hold-`T` wait (progress, exactly one hour, fresh press), out-of-time hint/label, battle still startable, hidden in battle, refilled next preparation.
- `GameStageLoopPlayTests.cs` (`B-9.3`): Game scene with the real stage assets (overrides the single-wave fixture). Plays every catalog stage (1→7 since CONTENT-01) back to back, killing each wave as it is released. At every preparation start it checks that only this stage's enemies exist (hidden), the wave timetable restarts, retreat records are empty, no arrows/cannon shells remain, ladders are off and empty, and morale battle state is closed. Each victory pays once (gold unchanged while on the reward screen and after moving on; `ContinueToNextStage` works once).
